## Acquisition positioning

**Status:** Owner-approved, 2026-09-01

> "Funūn attracts artists and record labels with the tools they need before releasing a song, or grabbing a sync opportunity."

**Intended use:** Early pitch-deck positioning that explains Funūn's creator-and-label
acquisition wedge before introducing The Crate and the sync monetization rail.

**Supporting doctrine:** The Crate is not the front door to Funūn. It is an opportunity
unlocked over time as artists and labels use the Writer's Room, Song Passport, master
storage, metadata, collaboration, split sheets and Contract Locker.

## The Funūn flywheel

**Status:** Owner-approved, 2026-09-01

**Suggested slide title:** The tools artists need today create tomorrow's opportunity

```text
Writer's Room
    ↓
Songs, versions and collaborators
    ↓
Song Passport + master storage
    ↓
Splits, contracts and rights information
    ↓
Background sync-readiness guidance
    ↓
Artist-controlled invitation into The Crate
    ↓
Curated pitching, licensing and revenue
```

**Slide interpretation:** Funūn earns artists and record labels through practical
creator infrastructure, not by requiring them to arrive with a sync opportunity. Their
normal use of the Writer's Room, Song Passport, master storage, metadata, collaboration,
split sheets and Contract Locker gradually creates a rights-ready catalogue. Appropriate
songs may then enter The Crate through a deliberate, artist-controlled and Funūn-curated
process.

**Key deck sentence:**

> The Crate is not the front door to Funūn. It is the opportunity unlocked by using Funūn well.

**Non-negotiable boundary:** Using the Writer's Room, Sound Vault or any creator tool
never automatically authorizes sync representation or places a song in The Crate.

## Approved public acquisition architecture

**Status:** Owner-approved, 2026-09-01

Funūn should lead publicly with the tools artists and record labels need before release
or sync. Sync readiness may develop quietly underneath normal product use, but The Crate
is introduced as an optional downstream opportunity rather than the acquisition promise.

### Problem-to-product mapping

| Artist or label need | Approved Funūn solution |
|---|---|
| A space to create songs | **The Writer's Room** |
| Song and master storage | **Sound Vault** |
| Metadata that follows the song | **Song Passport** |
| Split sheets and legal documents | **Contract Locker** |
| Collaboration and contributor records | **Sound Vault** |
| Release readiness | **Sound Vault** |
| Secure sharing and delivery | **Song Passport + Sound Vault** |
| Creative, legal and business information together | **Sound Vault + Contract Locker + Song Passport** |

These assignments are owner-approved. Do not silently move a capability to a different
named product during deck writing.

### The four public products

#### The Writer's Room - create the song

Start with a hum, lyric or take and work on the song with the people making it.

#### Sound Vault - store, organize and prepare

Keep songs, masters, versions, contributor records, release readiness and secure sharing
in one organized home.

#### Song Passport - carry the truth forward

Credits, rights, provenance and metadata follow the song from its first demo through
release and secure delivery.

#### Contract Locker - protect the business

Keep split sheets, agreements, registrations and signed legal records connected to the
music they govern.

### Umbrella message

> Funūn keeps the creative work, the master, the metadata and the paperwork connected - from the first idea to whatever comes next.

### What leads publicly

- The Writer's Room
- Sound Vault
- Song Passport
- Contract Locker
- Master and song organization
- Collaboration and contributor records
- Metadata continuity
- Split sheets and legal-document organization
- Release readiness
- Secure sharing and delivery

### What remains underneath at first

- Background sync-readiness guidance
- Internal curation and quality review
- The Crate catalogue-building process
- Matching and pitching operations
- Representation, licensing, delivery and payment infrastructure

These background systems should influence the data model now without making artists feel
that ordinary use of Funūn automatically enrolls their work into licensing.
