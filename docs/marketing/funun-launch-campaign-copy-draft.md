## Campaign 1: The B2B Workflow Angle

### “From Demo to Delivery”

### Asset 1: Website Landing Page Hero & Core Copy

#### AIDA Framework

**Attention**

# The song is finished. Why are you still chasing paperwork?

Missing splits. Wrong writer names. Masters buried in somebody’s Dropbox. A release date creeping closer while three collaborators still haven’t sent their PRO information.

That isn’t artist development. It’s administrative damage control.

**Interest**

Funūn connects the writing room to the release pipeline.

Writers and artists enter their identity, publishing, PRO, and payment details once. From there, the information stays connected to the work—so your team isn’t rebuilding the same song record from texts, spreadsheets, and half-completed forms every time the track moves forward.

You can see what’s ready, what’s missing, and who still needs to handle it.

**Desire**

One place for the pieces that usually live everywhere:

- High-resolution masters and stems in the Sound Vault
- Split sheets and agreements in the Contract Locker
- Writer, performer, publishing, and ownership details attached to the song
- Registration tasks tracked before they become release-day emergencies
- A cleaner path from demo approval to distribution, licensing, and sync review

When somebody asks for the clean master, final splits, or complete metadata, your team can send the right package—not start another email chain.

**Action**

## Bring one project into the beta.

See how Funūn fits your team before you move your whole catalog.

**CTA:** Request label and management beta access

**Supporting line:** No hard sell. We’ll walk through one real release and show you exactly where Funūn saves the chase.
