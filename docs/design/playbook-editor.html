First move — before you touch Vercel
Read the external uptime monitor. A Vercel-hosted check can't report a Vercel outage, so it's the only read that tells you whole-site vs. one route.
+⋮⋮
Use this when Vercel → Observability shows a 5xx spike or a FUNCTION_THROTTLED event. The goal is to confirm blast radius, find the cause, and decide roll-back vs. forward-fix.
