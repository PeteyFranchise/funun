# Intake Audit — Music Synchronization & Master Use Deal Memo

**Status:** Research only; not legal advice; not approved for generation, signature, or use as an operative agreement.  
**Source received:** 2026-10-04  
**Substantive source edits:** None

## Classification

The draft appears intended to combine synchronization and master-use permission in one short-form deal memo. That can fit a transaction where the same licensor controls both compositions and recordings, but the source does not establish what happens when those rights are controlled by different parties.

The title says “deal memo,” while the signature block and operative terms may indicate an intended binding agreement. That status must be made explicit before any production use.

## Legal-substance questions requiring counsel and owner decisions

- Is the memo binding when signed, or is it subject to a later long-form agreement?
- Does one Licensor represent and warrant control of both master and publishing rights? If not, should separate master and publishing instruments or signature blocks be required?
- Who may exercise each option, by what notice method, before what deadline, and when is payment due?
- Do option rights become effective on notice, payment, or both? What happens after the Initial Term expires?
- How are partial exercises, prior payments, taxes, withholding, refunds, late payment, and currency handled?
- What precisely is included in “associated online/social promotional trailers,” “home-video formats,” “broadcast,” and “all-media”?
- Are edits, excerpts, trailers, advertising, dubbing, localization, soundtrack albums, clips, derivatives, and new technologies included or excluded?
- Are public-performance royalties preserved, and who handles cue-sheet information and filings?
- What representations, warranties, indemnities, limitation-of-liability terms, notices, assignment rules, governing law, venue, force-majeure terms, confidentiality terms, and counterparts/e-sign provisions are required?
- Should the credit remedy, waiver of injunctive relief, Most Favored Nations comparison class, and worldwide perpetual options be narrowed or qualified?
- Does the producer need the right to assign the license to financiers, distributors, broadcasters, platforms, successors, or affiliates?
- What evidence establishes chain of title and authority for all writers, publishers, performers, labels, and master owners?

## Proposed conditional logic

- If master and publishing are not controlled by the same party, require separate rightsholder records and counsel-approved execution structure.
- If publishing ownership totals less than 100%, block any “fully cleared” representation.
- If any option is offered, require its fee, currency, scope, exercise deadline, notice method, and effective trigger.
- If promotional use is included, require platforms/media, clip limits, paid-media status, and term.
- If the work or recording has samples, interpolations, featured performers, union obligations, or third-party restrictions, require supporting clearance evidence or a recorded blocker.
- If protected language changes, remove any future counsel-reviewed designation until the exact version is reviewed again.
