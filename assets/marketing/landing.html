    01The Crate

    We shop it. You keep the rights.

    The Crate is Funūn’s curated sync catalogue. A spot in it is earned, not bought. Once a song is in, our team puts it in front of the people we work with: music supervisors, ad agencies, filmmakers, game studios and brands.

    • You submit, we review. We can’t see inside your Sound Vault until you send a song over. Submitting is free, and it’s what starts the relationship.
    • Admission is the earned part. No pitch to write. We judge the song on quality, and on demand for that kind of music across the projects coming our way. Splits, credits and metadata aren’t the test. Funūn walks you through getting those right either way.
    • Then we shop it. One agreement authorizes us to represent what you submit, with price and terms still negotiated per deal. When a brief fits, your song goes out in a Selects like this one. You still own your music. Representing it is not owning it, and nothing you submit changes who the song belongs to.
