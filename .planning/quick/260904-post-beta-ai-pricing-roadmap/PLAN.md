# Post-beta AI pricing roadmap note

## Objective

Record the owner-approved direction for a future Funūn freemium/paid program
and preserve the complete decision checklist for pricing AI-assisted tools after
beta evidence is available.

## Scope

- Add a visible future-discussion entry to the main roadmap.
- Create one authoritative deliberation brief covering OpenAI and Anthropic
  tools across Funūn, not only Lyric Lift.
- Keep tier prices, included allowances, and launch dates explicitly undecided.
- Preserve core songwriting and manual workflows when AI access or allowance is
  unavailable.
- Treat the individual Funūn user account as the initial entitlement and billing
  boundary; organization/team billing remains future scope.

## Files expected to change

- `.planning/ROADMAP.md`
- `.planning/deliberations/post-beta-ai-pricing-and-governance.md`
- `.planning/quick/260904-post-beta-ai-pricing-roadmap/SUMMARY.md`

## Validation plan

- Confirm the roadmap links to the deliberation brief.
- Confirm the brief covers tier principles, metering, ceilings, dashboards,
  economics, billing lifecycle, privacy/security, abuse controls, beta metrics,
  rollout gates, and open decisions.
- Run Markdown/link/path and whitespace checks appropriate to a docs-only change.

## Risks and coordination notes

- Do not set customer prices or promise usage quantities before beta data exists.
- Do not describe AI as unlimited.
- Do not let paid AI become a prerequisite for basic creation, collaboration,
  rights entry, or access to an artist's own work.
- This is a planning-only update; it changes no production access or billing.
