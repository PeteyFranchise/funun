# Funūn-Owned Distributor Option

## Objective

Document what it would take for Funūn to operate a branded, managed and eventually
direct music-distribution business, while clearly distinguishing a partner-powered
service from a true direct DSP distributor.

## Scope

- Define three distribution operating models and the legal/technical meaning of each.
- Record the recommended partner-powered-to-direct progression.
- Capture DSP qualification, contracting, identifiers, DDEX, custody, quality control,
  fraud, rights, royalty accounting, payments, tax, support and staffing obligations.
- Define staged gates, evidence, planning estimates and stop/go decisions.
- Preserve the existing distributor-API partner evaluation as the fastest first lane.
- Keep counsel review, external applications, partner outreach and commercial commitments
  owner-controlled.

## Validation Plan

- Confirm partner-powered, managed-hybrid and direct-distributor models are not conflated.
- Confirm every material business, legal, technical, financial and operational function
  has an explicit owner or gate.
- Confirm official Apple, Spotify, DDEX, ISRC, GS1, Merlin and YouTube evidence is linked.
- Confirm the document does not claim Funūn is currently a distributor or direct DSP partner.
- Confirm the recommended path preserves Song Passport, Release Report and Sound Vault doctrine.
- Run `git diff --check` on every changed planning file.

## Risks / Coordination Notes

- Direct distribution is an ongoing contractual, financial, compliance and operational
  business, not a one-time integration feature.
- DSP acceptance and direct commercial agreements are discretionary and cannot be promised.
- Royalty custody, payments, tax, sanctions, rights disputes and fraud require qualified
  legal, accounting and compliance review before launch.
- Timelines are internal planning ranges, not vendor or DSP commitments.
- No external outreach, application, account creation or commercial commitment is
  authorized by this documentation task.
- Existing unrelated worktree changes belong to the user and will not be modified.
