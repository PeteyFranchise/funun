# Funūn-Owned Distributor Option - Summary

## What Changed

- Added Funūn Distribution to the roadmap as a long-term strategic option.
- Defined three distinct models: partner-powered branded distribution, a managed hybrid,
  and selective direct relationships with named DSPs.
- Recommended a gated progression from partner-powered to hybrid to direct instead of
  attempting a universal direct-distribution launch.
- Documented the corporate/legal, DSP, rights, identifiers, DDEX, delivery, royalty,
  payout, fraud, support, staffing and business-model capabilities Funūn would need.
- Added staged evidence gates, internal planning ranges, a cost-model checklist, launch
  requirements, success measures, a truthful claim ladder and ten later discussion decisions.
- Linked the owned-distributor option from the existing API-partner evaluation and DDEX
  production-readiness deliberation.
- Preserved the strategic rule that Song Passport, Release Report and Sound Vault remain
  canonical and provider-neutral throughout the transition.

## Validation Run

- Confirmed branded, managed-hybrid and direct-distributor models are clearly separated.
- Confirmed direct claims require a named DSP agreement and production acceptance.
- Confirmed royalty custody, payouts, tax, rights, fraud and Content ID require separate
  professional review and operating controls.
- Confirmed official Apple, Spotify, DDEX, ISRC, GS1, Merlin and YouTube sources are linked.
- Confirmed the existing Too Lost/Revelator/FUGA/SonoSuite evaluation remains the first
  practical partner-powered path.
- Ran `git diff --check` on the changed planning files; no whitespace errors were reported.
- No application code, database schema or production behavior changed, so no code test or
  migration was required.

## Remaining Risks / Follow-ups

- The owner and team must decide whether distribution is central, optional or out of scope.
- Music-distribution counsel and qualified accounting/compliance professionals must map
  the intended rights and money flows before a launch model is approved.
- Current vendor and DSP requirements must be refreshed before any application or outreach.
- The 3-6, 6-18 and 18-36+ month ranges are internal planning estimates, not commitments.
- A five-year unit model and written pilot budget are required before partner selection.
- Direct DSP acceptance is discretionary; a partner-powered service may remain the best
  permanent economic and operational model.
