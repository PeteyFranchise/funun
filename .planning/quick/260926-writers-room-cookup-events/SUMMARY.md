# Summary — Writer's Room Cookups, audience, broadcasting and host commerce

**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Updated:** 2026-09-27  
**Workflow:** `/gsd-quick` manual fallback  
**Result:** product direction recorded; no application code or migration changed

## What changed

- Expanded the Cookups brief from scheduling/cards/guest entry into a staged product program.
- Recorded separate host/co-host, creative-seat and viewer-pass concepts.
- Recorded free, paid and mixed admission, including complimentary and application/invite cases.
- Added Open Studio as a free or paid broad-audience format backed by a small interactive stage and
  scalable viewer broadcast.
- Added optional social simulcast through a curated program output, with full, preview,
  Funūn-only and no-external-stream policies.
- Added consent, privacy, emergency-control and paid-stream leakage boundaries.
- Added the paid-admission doctrine: transparent fees, one initial payout owner, provider-held bank
  credentials, immutable order terms, a reconciled internal ledger, explicit money states and
  refund/dispute/payout controls.
- Recorded Studio ($19/month) as the core Writer's Room video and Cookup-hosting unlock while
  keeping participation available to Writer-tier Members and account-free guests.
- Recorded Team ($49/month) as the scale/operations tier rather than the first hosting gate, with a
  cost-gated 3% transaction-fee hypothesis; Studio's standard planning fee is 5%.
- Kept exact minutes, stage size and viewer caps open until provider costs are measured.
- Added Studio-level participant cameras and shared-screen/DAW presentation, plus Team-level paired
  secondary angles, co-host switching and saved scenes.
- Added the camera-only secondary-device pairing model and the no-echo microphone/speaker defaults.
- Separated source connection, creative-stage visibility, viewer-program selection, external
  simulcast and recording so consent or activation cannot cascade silently.
- Added browser/system-audio verification, high-quality music audio and direct artifact exchange as
  layered DAW fallbacks rather than promising universal application-audio capture.
- Added a focused Cookups by Funūn audience destination/PWA for Discover, Following, Upcoming,
  Live now, tickets, authorized replays and notification preferences.
- Preserved guest attendance and lightweight audience account claim without forcing creative
  onboarding; host/series follows remain separate from professional and rights relationships.
- Kept one Funūn identity, backend, event marketplace, payment ledger and follower graph, with a
  separate native viewer shell gated on measured audience/mobile value.
- Created the canonical Cookups Product Doctrine with 28 permanent principles, a capability-truth
  warning, changeable planning-parameter section, phase compliance checklist and Playbook
  publication rule.
- Linked the doctrine from the Cookups brief, roadmap, organizational-doctrine index and Playbook
  publication map under Company-wide → Standards & Doctrine.
- Preserved the separation between ticket economics, event roles, contribution, credit, ownership
  and splits.
- Restructured rollout as event identity/cards, live studio/handoff, viewer broadcast/simulcast,
  paid commerce/payouts and later marketplace maturity.
- Updated the Writer's Room live-collaboration todo and project roadmap to point planners to the
  expanded brief.
