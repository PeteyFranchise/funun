# Seven-Capability Roadmap Visual

## Objective

Create a polished, one-page PDF roadmap that presents seven future Funun capabilities in a credible sequence and uses the product's dark indigo/fuchsia visual system.

## Scope

- Organize all seven capabilities into near-term, product-roadmap, and partner-dependent lanes.
- Give each capability a plain-language outcome and practical dependency.
- Include a recommended two-item focus for the next planning session.
- Produce and visually verify one final PDF.

## Files Expected to Change

- `output/pdf/funun-seven-capability-roadmap.pdf`
- `tmp/pdfs/` builder and rendered QA files
- `.planning/quick/260901-seven-capability-roadmap/PLAN.md`
- `.planning/quick/260901-seven-capability-roadmap/SUMMARY.md`

## Validation Plan

- Reopen the PDF and verify page count and text extraction.
- Render the final PDF to PNG with Poppler.
- Inspect the rendered page for clipping, overlap, legibility, alignment, and color fidelity.

## Risks / Coordination Notes

- "Coming soon" can imply a delivery commitment. The visual will distinguish delivery lanes rather than assigning unsupported dates.
- Direct delivery, Content ID, collections, and payments depend on external partners and legal/commercial decisions.
- No application code or product roadmap source-of-truth files will be changed.
