# Seven-Capability Roadmap Visual - Summary

## What Changed

- Created a one-page Funun product roadmap PDF using the application's ink, card, lavender, indigo, fuchsia, emerald and amber palette.
- Sequenced all seven capabilities across four honest delivery categories: Activate next, Build next, Partner to scale and Decide commercially.
- Added a recommended deep-dive on metadata continuity and the counsel-ready sync framework.
- Added PDF title, author and subject metadata.

## Validation Run

- Reopened the PDF with `pdfinfo`: one landscape page, valid PDF 1.4, no encryption or scripting.
- Extracted the document with `pdfplumber`: all seven capability headings are present.
- Rendered the final PDF at 144 DPI and visually inspected it for clipping, overlap, alignment, contrast and legibility.
- Corrected the capability-card body spacing after the first render and revalidated the final render.

## Remaining Risks / Follow-ups

- The roadmap intentionally does not assign dates until the selected capabilities receive scoped plans, owners and dependencies.
- Direct delivery and money/protection rails remain partner-dependent.
- The founder-label plan remains a commercial policy decision, not solely a product build.
