# Plan — Nigil Wednesday Meeting Brief

## Scope

Create a Google Docs-compatible meeting report that translates the owner-approved
Funūn product plan into a truthful, practical 45-minute meeting with Nigil Mack.
The report will distinguish what works today, what is coming soon, what is on the
roadmap, and what depends on partner exploration. It will avoid pitching direct
Funūn sync placement and instead focus on creative continuity, song registration,
release operations, pitching readiness, and general sync readiness.

## Assumptions

- The meeting goal is a small operational pilot, not a platform-wide label rollout.
- Secretly remains the delivery/registration destination; Funūn currently prepares
  the information and handoff but does not auto-submit or claim partner acceptance.
- Product claims must be limited to capabilities evidenced in the current codebase
  or explicitly approved roadmap documents.
- The deliverable will be a DOCX optimized for import into Google Docs.

## Deliverable

- `output/documents/Funun-Nigil-Wednesday-Meeting-Battle-Plan.docx`
- Includes an executive brief, claim ladder, current and future workflows, exact
  click paths, minute-by-minute facilitation plan, pilot design, talk tracks,
  language guardrails, and page-by-page AI pitch-deck instructions.

## Verification

1. Cross-check every current-state claim against routes/components in the repo.
2. Cross-check future-state language against `.planning/ROADMAP.md` and approved
   deliberation/TODO artifacts.
3. Sanitize the Google Docs title block.
4. Render the DOCX to page images and inspect every page for clipping, overlap,
   broken tables, awkward page breaks, and inconsistent hierarchy.
