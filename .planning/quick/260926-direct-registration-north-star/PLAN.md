# Plan — Direct registration, publishing and distribution north star

**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Type:** Documentation/doctrine update (`/gsd-quick` manual fallback)

## Objective

Record the owner's long-term direction that Funūn should minimize avoidable middlemen and
develop toward three distinct capabilities: acting as a publisher/administrator for works it
represents, acting as a distributor, and submitting registrations for works it does not publish
when the rights holders have expressly authorized that service.

## Scope

- Add the north star and staged operating model to `.planning/ROADMAP.md`.
- Update the pending CWR sender business-track todo so it covers direct society registration,
  explicit third-party authority, and its relationship to the publisher and distributor tracks.
- Reconcile `docs/cwr-plan.md` with the owner-approved long-term operating model so its old
  admin-only recommendation is understood as a starting sequence, not the destination.
- Correct the adjacent PRO-identity cross-reference todo where it compresses recipient/society
  onboarding into the imprecise phrase “CISAC-affiliated publisher.”
- Add a durable doctrine document that future product, data-model, UI and integration plans must
  cite.
- Extend Song Passport doctrine so canonical rights data can support those future roles without
  conflating publishing ownership, administration authority, registration authority or
  distribution authority.
- Add the direction to `.planning/PROJECT.md` so it survives milestone transitions.
- Add a short doctrine pointer outside the managed project blocks in `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and
  `AGENTS.md`, so Claude and Codex receive the same direction without replacing unrelated generated
  or project-specific instructions.

## Guardrails

- Describe a planned end state, never a capability that Funūn currently has.
- Prefer direct relationships and portable standards, while allowing transitional partners where
  legal, commercial or recipient onboarding requires them.
- Keep the three business roles legally and technically separable.
- Never treat a contributor's data, a split-sheet signature or platform membership as authority
  for Funūn to register, administer, distribute or collect income.
- Require immutable submission snapshots, explicit mandates, acknowledgements, corrections,
  revocations and audit history.
- Keep migration and production changes out of this documentation-only task.
