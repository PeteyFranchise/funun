Health rules leadership only

Health color reflects the client's last actual license — are they still buying? The scale runs Good → Warning → At-risk → Cold (licensed before but gone quiet far longer than at-risk — a client who may have drifted or had a bad experience). Brand-new prospects (no license yet) get a symbol, not a color. Last contact and time in stage are tracked separately (shown as columns + in the log) but don't set the color. Computed live.
