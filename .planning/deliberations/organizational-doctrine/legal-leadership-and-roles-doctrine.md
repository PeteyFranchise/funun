# Funūn Legal Leadership and Roles Doctrine

**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED DRAFT — 2026-09-12
**Intended home:** The Playbook → Rights, Legal & Contract Operations → Legal Leadership
**Audience:** Leadership, Legal, Rights Operations, Contract Operations, Finance, Sales, Sync, Product and other specifically authorized Funūn Team Members
**Purpose:** Define when Funūn establishes internal legal leadership, what each legal role owns, and where licensed legal judgment ends and operational work begins.

## Working definition

Funūn Legal protects the company's ability to serve creators, team members and Client Partners lawfully, fairly and sustainably. It provides or coordinates licensed legal judgment; preserves privilege; advises the company on risk; supports transactions and disputes; and helps Leadership make informed business decisions.

Legal does not own every decision that contains legal risk. The accountable business leader still owns the business decision, Finance owns financial controls, Product owns implementation, Rights Operations owns rights evidence, and Contract Operations owns document workflow. Legal identifies the legal boundaries, advises on consequences, approves where delegated authority requires it, and refuses or escalates work the company cannot lawfully or ethically perform.

## Governing principles

- A title, system role, training completion or access grant does not make someone a lawyer or authorize legal advice.
- Roles requiring licensed legal judgment must be held by an attorney authorized to practice in the relevant jurisdiction or supervised through approved counsel.
- Legal advice to Funūn represents Funūn. It does not automatically represent an artist, songwriter, collaborator, buyer, employee or other platform user.
- Privileged matters, legal holds, investigations and attorney work product must not be placed in general CRM notes, Playbook articles or broadly accessible operational records.
- Legal risk and business risk are related but distinct. Legal advises; the properly authorized business leader decides unless law, contract or delegated policy requires Legal approval.
- Revenue urgency never overrides consent, ownership conflicts, sanctions, court orders, privacy duties, employment protections, financial controls or binding contractual restrictions.
- Outside counsel extends specialist capacity; it does not erase the need for a named internal owner.
- Rights claims, evidence, verification, clearance, legal conclusions and adjudicated outcomes remain separate states.

## Function boundaries

| Function | Primary responsibility | Must not do by implication |
|---|---|---|
| Legal | Licensed advice, negotiation, privilege, disputes, regulatory interpretation, legal holds and exceptional approval | Operate every routine document or metadata workflow |
| Rights Operations | Record claims, contributors, registrations, authority evidence, conflicts and clearance dependencies | Decide contested ownership or provide legal advice |
| Contract Operations | Maintain approved templates, generation, signing, versions, notices, obligations and immutable originals | Create unapproved legal language or interpret a dispute |
| Sync & Licensing | Operate briefs, commercial scope, clearance coordination, negotiation handoffs and delivery | Promise rights or terms outside documented authority |
| Finance | Maintain pricing controls, invoices, collections, allocations, payouts, tax coordination and reconciliation | Treat legal review as financial approval or vice versa |
| Leadership | Own company strategy, risk appetite and reserved business decisions | Override law, conceal material risk or manufacture legal approval |

## Leadership structure by stage

### Beta and first-ten stage

Funūn uses approved outside or fractional counsel for music, commercial, privacy, employment, corporate and other specialist matters. The COO is the internal coordination owner: they ensure questions are routed, advice is recorded in the proper restricted location, deadlines are owned and decisions are implemented. The COO does not become legal counsel by holding this responsibility.

The CEO remains accountable for company decisions and counsel engagement. No employee should present operational guidance as a legal conclusion. A current counsel roster, engagement scope, conflict contact and emergency escalation path must exist before material licensing or employment activity scales.

### Operating-foundation stage

The first Rights & Contract Operations Specialist normally arrives before an internal attorney. That role reduces preventable legal work by maintaining evidence, approved workflows, obligations and escalation intake, but remains operational.

Funūn should hire its first **General Counsel** around 25–40 employees, and no later than the expansion toward ten AEs, unless the hiring gate is clearly unmet and approved outside counsel demonstrably provides equivalent coverage. The General Counsel reports to the CEO and has direct access to the Board or equivalent governing body for material legal, ethical or fiduciary concerns.

### Ten-AE stage

The legal function should ordinarily include:

- General Counsel.
- Commercial and Licensing Counsel.
- Rights/IP Counsel or a senior rights specialist operating under counsel-defined boundaries.
- Legal or Contract Operations support.
- Approved outside specialists for employment, litigation, international, tax, privacy or regulatory matters.

### Fifty-AE stage

A five-to-ten-person Legal function is expected, led by the General Counsel. Specialization may include commercial/licensing, rights and intellectual property, product/privacy/AI, employment, disputes and legal operations. Regional counsel is added only where actual jurisdictional workload requires it.

### One-hundred-AE global stage

A ten-to-eighteen-person global Legal function may justify a **Chief Legal Officer & General Counsel**. The CLO/GC leads legal strategy and governance while deputy, regional or specialty leads own defined practices. Geographic and subject-matter leaders must not create competing authority over the same matter; every matter has one accountable legal owner.

## Internal General Counsel hiring gate

Leadership should forecast the role 6–12 months ahead. Hire when several signals converge:

- Outside legal spending approaches the fully loaded cost of an appropriate internal leader.
- Legal review or negotiation repeatedly delays licences, enterprise agreements, partnerships or product launches.
- Legal demand consistently consumes roughly 60–75% of a full-time senior lawyer's capacity.
- Rights conflicts, claims, takedowns, disputes or exceptional negotiations become recurring rather than occasional.
- Funūn begins material multi-jurisdiction operations or regularly handles international agreements.
- Privacy, AI, employment, corporate, securities, consumer, royalty or regulatory exposure becomes operationally significant.
- Leadership needs routine privileged advice and institutional legal memory.
- The commercial team is expanding toward ten AEs and fulfilment complexity is growing with it.

Move the hire earlier after a material incident, financing, acquisition opportunity, regulatory inquiry or litigation threat. Do not postpone the role merely because outside counsel can react; the relevant question is whether Funūn needs continuous internal legal leadership.

## Job outlines

### General Counsel

**Mission:** Serve as Funūn's accountable internal legal leader and trusted adviser while building a legal function proportionate to the company's risk and stage.

**Core responsibilities:**

- Advise the CEO, COO, Leadership and governing body on material legal obligations and risk.
- Establish legal policy, matter intake, privilege, document retention, legal holds and outside-counsel standards.
- Lead or supervise material commercial, licensing, corporate, privacy, employment, regulatory and dispute matters.
- Define which agreements, deviations and incidents require Legal review or approval.
- Maintain one defensible legal position across teams without suppressing legitimate dissent or escalation.
- Build the legal team, counsel roster, budget and succession plan.
- Partner with Product and Security on privacy, AI, platform governance and incident response.

**Authority:** May issue legal advice for Funūn, retain approved outside counsel within delegated budget, protect privilege, require preservation, halt conduct reasonably believed unlawful, and escalate material risk directly to the CEO or governing body.

**Boundary:** The General Counsel does not unilaterally own ordinary product, creative, sales or financial decisions merely because they involve risk. They must distinguish legal requirements from recommendations and business preferences.

### Chief Legal Officer & General Counsel

**When appropriate:** A later-stage title for a global or materially complex organization; not an automatic promotion based on tenure or headcount.

**Additional responsibilities:**

- Lead enterprise legal strategy, governance and global regulatory posture.
- Advise the governing body on fiduciary, corporate and systemic risk.
- Coordinate regional and specialty legal leaders under one matter-ownership model.
- Oversee major disputes, investigations, acquisitions, financing and structural transactions.
- Ensure legal capacity scales with commercial expansion without becoming an opaque approval bureaucracy.

### Commercial and Licensing Counsel

**Mission:** Support lawful, clear and commercially usable transactions.

**Responsibilities:** Draft and negotiate approved commercial and licensing agreements; review non-standard terms; advise on licence scope, warranties, indemnities, limitations, exclusivity and remedies; support enterprise buyers and strategic relationships; and convert recurring negotiated positions into counsel-approved templates and playbooks.

**Boundary:** Sales owns the relationship and commercial objective. Sync operates the deal. Legal controls legal interpretation and approved deviations. Counsel must never fabricate authority or treat a CRM stage as proof of rights.

### Rights and Intellectual Property Counsel

**Mission:** Advise on copyright, neighbouring rights, trademarks, publicity rights, catalogue authority and contested claims.

**Responsibilities:** Review rights conflicts and complex clearance; advise on ownership, control, samples, adaptations, takedowns and claims; define escalation standards; guide Rights Operations; and coordinate specialist or local counsel where jurisdiction requires it.

**Boundary:** Counsel does not rewrite historical evidence to reach a convenient result. Conflicting assertions remain preserved even when the company decides what action it may take.

### Product, Privacy and AI Counsel

**Mission:** Help Product and IT build lawful, explainable and appropriately controlled services.

**Responsibilities:** Advise on privacy, data use, retention, consent, automated systems, AI providers, model inputs and outputs, platform terms, accessibility-related legal obligations, security incidents and regulatory change; participate early in high-risk product design; and maintain jurisdictional guidance with the accountable product owner.

**Boundary:** Legal review is not a substitute for threat modelling, security engineering, accessibility testing, product research or ethical judgment.

### Employment Counsel

**Mission:** Advise Funūn on lawful and fair team-member practices.

**Responsibilities:** Support employment agreements, classification, workplace policies, accommodations, investigations, discipline, separation, benefits and multi-jurisdiction employment obligations in partnership with TMS.

**Boundary:** TMS owns the employee experience and operational process. Legal protects process integrity and advises on legal risk; it must not turn every people matter into adversarial litigation management.

### Legal Operations Team Lead

**Mission:** Make the legal function organized, measurable, secure and usable without exercising unsupervised legal judgment.

**Responsibilities:** Own matter intake, routing, deadlines, approved systems, counsel billing, templates, knowledge operations, legal-vendor administration, reporting and access reviews; coordinate Legal SLAs; and maintain separation between privileged files and general operational records.

**Boundary:** Legal Operations may administer a legal decision but may not manufacture, alter or independently issue one.

### Contract Operations Specialist

**Mission:** Preserve accurate, complete and executable agreement workflows.

**Responsibilities:** Use approved templates; coordinate generation and signature; maintain version and signer integrity; track notices, expirations and obligations; preserve immutable originals; and escalate requested deviations or ambiguous authority.

**Boundary:** The specialist may explain the workflow and approved form fields, not interpret disputed language or provide individualized legal advice.

### Rights Operations Specialist

**Mission:** Maintain reliable rights evidence and clearance dependencies.

**Responsibilities:** Record claims, contributors, registrations, permissions, restrictions and provenance; identify missing or conflicting evidence; coordinate routine clearance; and escalate contested or exceptional matters.

**Boundary:** Verification confirms evidence for a scoped fact. It does not adjudicate ownership, cure defective authority or guarantee licensability.

### Paralegal or Legal Coordinator

**Mission:** Extend supervised legal and operational capacity.

**Responsibilities:** Support matter files, factual research, document preparation, signature packets, deadlines, filings and counsel coordination under attorney supervision where required.

**Boundary:** The role does not provide independent legal advice or approve matters reserved to counsel.

## Matter ownership and escalation

Every legal matter records one accountable legal owner, one business owner, the affected parties, privilege classification, jurisdiction, severity, deadline, decision status and approved audience. Handoffs are not complete until the receiving owner accepts them.

Escalate immediately when a matter involves threatened or active litigation, government inquiry, subpoena or court order; credible rights infringement; material data exposure; sanctions or fraud concerns; employee safety or retaliation; a disputed ownership claim affecting a live transaction; a requested concealment or record alteration; or conduct that may be unlawful.

The General Counsel may require a legal hold or pause the affected activity. Only the authorized legal owner may close the legal question; only the authorized business owner may accept the remaining business risk.

## Service Level Agreements

Legal SLAs are triage commitments, not promised final-resolution times:

- **Level 1 — Emergency:** Immediate acknowledgement and active coordination for safety, court orders, active compromise, material illegality or deadline-critical exposure.
- **Level 2 — Urgent:** Same-business-day triage for live deals, disputes, regulatory contacts, threatened claims or material contractual deadlines.
- **Level 3 — Standard:** Prioritized review window for routine agreements, product questions, policies and non-urgent rights matters.
- **Level 4 — Planned:** Scheduled work for template improvement, training, research, audits and long-range regulatory readiness.

The General Counsel sets measurable response targets based on actual staffing and may publish narrower matter-specific SLAs. A fast acknowledgement must never be reported as a resolved matter.

## Console and record requirements

The Legal workspace should support restricted matter intake, conflict checks, one legal and one business owner, jurisdiction, privilege classification, deadlines, legal holds, approval matrices, outside counsel, spend, linked-but-separated operational records, immutable decision history and access review.

General Playbook doctrine may explain standards and escalation paths. It must never expose privileged advice, investigation details, employee medical information, sensitive disputes, raw identity documents, private counsel communications or unrestricted legal-matter lists.

## Metrics and anti-gaming safeguards

Measure response reliability, matter aging, recurring-risk reduction, outside-counsel effectiveness, template adoption, obligation completion, preventable disputes, incident learning and stakeholder clarity.

Do not reward Legal for blocking the most work, closing matters prematurely, minimizing reported risk, suppressing escalation or achieving a low outside-counsel bill at the expense of competent advice. Do not reward commercial teams for bypassing Legal or disguising non-standard terms as routine.

## Conflicts, independence and refusal

Legal Team Members disclose personal, financial and professional conflicts and recuse where necessary. The General Counsel must have a protected path to the CEO and governing body and may not be retaliated against for good-faith legal or ethical escalation.

Legal must refuse to facilitate unauthorized licensing, concealment, retaliation, falsified evidence, deceptive contracting, unlawful surveillance, misuse of privileged access or any transaction for which Funūn lacks the required authority. The refusal, responsible escalation and final authorized decision must be recorded in the proper restricted system.

## Governing legal leadership principle

> Legal helps Funūn move with informed courage: protecting creators, clients, team members and the company without confusing thoughtful control with unnecessary obstruction.
