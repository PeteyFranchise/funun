# Funūn Functional Team Doctrines

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-09-07
**Authority:** Internal operating doctrine and product-design input

## 1. A&R Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

A&R owns continuity of the creative relationship—from discovery through development, readiness, opportunity and follow-through. An A&R learns what a creator is building before recommending what happens next. The role does not take ownership of the creator's voice, promise selection or placement, adjudicate rights, or provide unauthorized legal, tax or financial advice.

### Core responsibilities

- Discover artists, writers, producers, songs and creative communities using judgment broader than follower counts or streaming totals.
- Understand works in progress, creative goals, team structure and catalogue priorities over time.
- Give specific, respectful feedback while preserving creator autonomy.
- Conduct contextual onboarding and help members begin with a real project rather than a rigid tour.
- Maintain catalogue awareness without allowing incomplete administration to obstruct creation.
- Match credible opportunities across the permissioned Funūn network—not only an assigned roster.
- Cultivate working relationships with AEs and understand upcoming buyer needs, constraints, rights requirements and deadlines.
- Circulate opportunities broadly enough to be fair, but narrowly enough to remain genuinely relevant.
- Facilitate consent-based introductions and distinguish collaboration from credit, ownership and project access.
- Encourage rights and business readiness, document useful context, close communication loops and escalate conflicts.

### Authority, workflow and console

A&R may recommend creators, projects, collaborators and next actions within delegated scope. Selection, contract, pricing, rights, verification, payment and exceptional access decisions stay with their authorized functions. The console must show member goals, relationship history, projects, catalogue context, permissioned opportunities, matching requirements, consideration records, submissions, feedback and follow-up. It must separate internal notes, member-visible messages and restricted escalations.

### SLAs, metrics and ethics

A&R SLAs measure acknowledgement, promised follow-up and closure of communication loops; they do not force artificial creative deadlines. Success includes relationship health, useful introductions, relevant opportunity coverage, readiness improvement, follow-through and member trust. Submission count, follower size and personal taste cannot become proxy measures for worth. Conflicts, favoritism, side solicitation, retaliation and misuse of unreleased work are prohibited.

## 2. Account Executive and Sales Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Account Executives own the day-to-day Client Partner relationship after acceptance, translate qualified demand into durable accounts and coordinate the commercial path. They do not promise clearance, legal approval, price exceptions, exclusivity, delivery or placement outside delegated authority.

### Core responsibilities

- Accept qualified BDT handoffs and preserve origin, relationship and promise history.
- Learn the buyer's organization, decision process, productions, briefs, budgets, timelines and communication preferences.
- Maintain an accurate book of business, relationship plan, next action, forecast and risk record.
- Coordinate briefs and opportunities with Sync and A&R without exposing restricted buyer information.
- Set realistic expectations and ensure handoffs are explicitly accepted—not merely sent.
- Develop account value through useful service, recurring workflow and trust rather than pressure.
- Carry buyer feedback back into catalogue, product and member-learning systems.
- Maintain continuity plans so institutional relationships are not trapped with one employee.

### Sales organization

The approved ladder is SVP of Sales → AVPs of Sales → Senior AEs → AEs → AAEs. An AAE works with an AE whose book justifies assistance, may own smaller accounts and must receive visible contribution credit. The supporting AE provides daily coaching; the AVP owns workload, performance and advancement. Funūn hires the first dedicated AE before the first AAE.

### SLAs, metrics and ethics

Measure qualified revenue, collected revenue, retention, forecast accuracy, client outcomes, service quality and clean handoffs. Keep gross licensing value, Funūn net revenue and rights-holder payouts separate. No lead-count-only compensation, duplicate ownership, hidden promises, artificial urgency or bypass of rights, legal, finance, verification or safety controls.

## 3. Business Development Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

BDT creates qualified institutional demand by developing Client Partner organizations and music buyers. Labels, publishers, distributors, creative service providers and artist-development relationships remain primarily A&R-owned. Technology and platform partnerships belong to Product/IT and Leadership; brand sponsorships and company-level strategic partnerships belong to Marketing and Leadership.

### Core responsibilities

- Identify and responsibly approach legitimate music buyers and Client Partner organizations.
- Research the organization, contacts, authority, use cases, likely demand and fit.
- Qualify the relationship without granting protected access or overstating Funūn's capabilities.
- Record source, evidence, needs, risks, communications and next steps.
- Prepare an accepted handoff to the appropriate AE or receiving function.
- Remain relationship sponsor and joint owner for approximately six months while the AE becomes primary day-to-day owner immediately.
- Preserve permanent origin attribution and complete a documented 30-, 90- and 180-day transition.

### Verification and organic buyers

BDT may recommend Client Partner verification. Only explicitly authorized senior BDT members, Leadership or a designated Verification function may decide it. A legitimate organic buyer ready for an eligible transaction must not be blocked by full organizational verification: use transaction-scoped identity, authority, rights, commercial, fraud, sanctions, signature and payment checks. Continuing organization privileges still require the appropriate verification.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure qualified relationships, accepted handoffs, retained accounts, source quality, conversion quality and post-handoff health. Do not reward raw contact volume, unverifiable introductions, indiscriminate outreach or premature verification. One organization may have several relationships, each with its own purpose and owner.

## 4. Talent Services and Member Success Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Talent Services and Member Success coordinates ongoing artist and member support, career gameplans, service delivery and retention. This relationship does not automatically make Funūn the member's legal manager, agent, fiduciary, publisher, label or attorney.

### Responsibilities

- Own welcoming, onboarding continuity and member gameplans.
- Help members identify priorities and select useful Funūn services without coercion.
- Coordinate specialists while preserving one visible relationship owner.
- Track commitments, milestones, risks, consent and follow-up.
- Explain what can be completed later and what becomes necessary for a particular opportunity.
- Surface product friction, unmet needs, retention risks and service-quality patterns.
- Maintain **Funūn Now**: available now, coming soon, current focus, opportunities, important updates and “you asked—we built.” Content must be personalized, curated, dismissible and expiring; profile roles may inform relevance but never grant permissions.

### Workflow, metrics and ethics

The console needs member goals, gameplans, services, responsible teams, tasks, communications, consent, risks and outcomes. Measure meaningful progress, service completion, retention, trust and closed loops—not dependency or time spent inside the product. Creative workflows stay optional and flexible. Members may decline services, change direction and begin with a simple voice-note idea.

## 5. Sync and Licensing Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Sync and Licensing operates qualified music-use opportunities from brief through clearance, agreement, delivery and post-placement follow-through. It coordinates decisions; it does not invent rights, override authorization or treat a pitch as a licence.

### Responsibilities and lifecycle

- Intake and normalize the brief, production, buyer, use, media, territory, term, budget and deadline.
- Identify and present eligible exact versions with accurate provenance.
- Keep creative fit, catalogue readiness, rights clearance, commercial approval, agreement, delivery, payment and cue-sheet status as separate facts.
- Coordinate A&R, Catalogue, Rights, Legal, AE and Finance through explicit stage gates.
- Preserve the selected version, approvals, negotiation history, delivery authorization and final use.
- Confirm use, collect cue-sheet information and route payment and reporting follow-up.

### The Crate readiness doctrine

Every version in The Crate carries a current state: one-stop pre-cleared, pre-cleared within parameters, fast-clearance available, or restricted/on hold. Eligibility requires exact-version identity, authority evidence, permitted uses, approval requirements, territory, term, pricing rules, delivery assets, attestation dates and expirations. Material change or expiration triggers an automatic hold until reverified.

### Cue Sheet Builder

The planned Cue Sheet Builder serves an entire production, including Funūn and outside music. It auto-fills from The Crate, Sound Vault, splits, profiles, rights and licences; supports manual/imported outside music, timecodes, usage types, collaborators, amendments and exports; and provides a restricted Cue Sheet Inbox for external metadata requests. Access may come through verified organization membership, production invitation or a restricted link. Beta use should collect feedback before final paid packaging.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure qualified briefs, appropriate submissions, clearance accuracy, turnaround, conversion, delivery quality, cue-sheet completion and rights-holder outcomes. Do not optimize pitch volume, conceal restrictions or permit urgency to erase consent and authority.

## 6. Rights, Legal and Contract Operations Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

This function preserves evidence, agreements, obligations and lawful escalation. Rights Operations, Contract Operations and Legal are related but distinct. Operational guidance is not legal advice, and a workflow status is not an adjudication of ownership.

### Responsibilities

- Rights Operations records claims, contributors, registrations, authority evidence, conflicts and clearance dependencies.
- Contract Operations governs approved templates, document generation, signing workflows, immutable originals, versions, notices and obligations.
- Legal provides or coordinates licensed legal judgment, negotiation, disputes, privilege and exceptional approvals.
- Keep claim, document, signature, agreement, obligation, registration and legal-matter statuses separate.
- Preserve who asserted, supplied, reviewed, approved, signed or changed each fact.
- Escalate conflicting claims and prevent the system from silently resolving them.

### Console, metrics and ethics

The console requires queues for missing evidence, signing, expirations, obligations, disputes and legal review with strict visibility. Measure accuracy, turnaround, avoided expiration, obligation completion and defensible records—not agreement volume or denial rate. AI may assist extraction and drafting but never deliver final legal judgment, waive rights or approve disputed ownership.

## 7. Catalogue, Metadata and Verification Operations Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Catalogue Operations makes people, works, recordings, versions, credits, metadata and delivery assets reliably identifiable and usable. Verification confirms evidence for a scoped fact; it does not adjudicate ownership or confer broad trust everywhere.

### Responsibilities

- Keep person identity, organization identity, contributor linking, work identity, recording identity and version identity distinct.
- Record field-level provenance, supplier, verification state, date and reviewer.
- Review credits, identifiers, assets, formats, metadata completeness and destination readiness.
- Maintain Crate eligibility and recurring attestations for exact versions.
- Offer accessible alternative evidence paths and respect names, aliases, diacritics and professional identities.
- Escalate rights conflicts rather than deciding them through metadata review.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure correction rates, provenance coverage, readiness accuracy, review time, downstream acceptance and recurring attestation health. Automated suggestions remain unverified until the proper evidence and authority standard is met. Never turn verification into prestige, identity policing or an unnecessary barrier to safe transactions.

## 8. Finance, Accounting and Royalties Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Finance ensures every dollar is connected to authority, evidence, accounting treatment and the correct recipient. Projected, contracted, invoiced, collected, allocated, payable and paid are different states.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain invoices, collections, commissions, fees, allocations, reserves, payable balances, statements and reconciliation.
- Keep currency explicit and use fixed-precision monetary calculations.
- Separate company revenue, transaction GMV and participant funds.
- Require strong authentication and independent review for payout-detail changes and material disbursements.
- Preserve source agreements, allocation logic, adjustments, approvals and immutable financial history.
- Operate exceptions, disputes, tax-document coordination and close processes.

### Leadership, metrics and ethics

Use CFO/Controller as the early combined financial authority, whether employed, fractional or externally supported. Measure collection quality, reconciliation, statement accuracy, close health, exception aging and payment timeliness. Never use participant money as unrestricted operating cash or compensate staff for delaying approvals or payments.

## 9. Marketing, Community and Audience Development Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Marketing helps the right people understand Funūn, supports member education, builds ethical audiences and creates useful community programming. It does not manufacture engagement, appropriate member stories or turn operational notices into hype.

### Responsibilities

- Develop positioning, campaigns, education, announcements and lifecycle communication.
- Operate member-facing community programming separately from TMS's internal employee engagement.
- Govern Funūn Now with Product, Member Success and responsible functional teams.
- Obtain asset- and purpose-specific consent for member stories, music, likeness and results.
- Track campaign source, audience, promise, expiry and accountable owner.
- Route sponsorship and company-level brand relationships with Leadership.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure qualified awareness, consented participation, useful engagement, conversion quality, retention contribution and trust. Prohibit dark patterns, deceptive scarcity, purchased vanity, undisclosed sponsorship, permanent consent assumptions and claims that exceed verified product reality.

## 10. Product, Engineering and IT Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Product, Engineering and IT own safe, reliable and responsible technical capability. Technical access does not grant business approval power, and production access exists only for a scoped operational need.

### Responsibilities

- Own discovery, requirements, design, engineering, quality, accessibility, releases, reliability, security, integrations and incident response.
- Keep initiatives, requirements, engineering work, releases, flags, incidents, vulnerabilities, access, integrations, AI use, environments and migrations as distinct records.
- Require reviewable authorization, least privilege, audit history and time-bounded support access.
- Protect creative work, rights, contracts, payments and identity data through architecture—not policy alone.
- Maintain provider inventories, AI cost attribution, account-wide spending ceilings, quality monitoring, human-review boundaries and non-AI fallback paths.
- Preserve the Writer's Room as a creative space without keystroke, productivity or surveillance tracking.

### Level 1–4 Occurrence Model

- **Level 1 — Critical:** active compromise, material data exposure, integrity failure or widespread production outage.
- **Level 2 — High:** major user impact, severe regression, failed critical integration or release requiring urgent coordinated response.
- **Level 3 — Moderate:** limited operational degradation, recoverable defect or contained risk needing scheduled remediation.
- **Level 4 — Low:** minor user impact or low-risk defect suitable for routine handling.

Severity and business priority remain separate. Planned releases, ordinary technical debt and routine maintenance are not occurrences unless a failure or active risk exists.

### Operating principle

Planned is not promised. Implemented is not verified. Deployed is not healthy. Measure reliability, security, accessibility, recovery, change quality and user outcomes—not output volume.

## 11. Leadership Doctrine

### Purpose and structure

Leadership sets doctrine, delegates authority, allocates resources, resolves escalations and remains accountable for organizational conduct. Approved early roles include Founder & CEO, COO, VP of Product & Technology, VP of Music & Creative Operations and CFO/Controller, with external legal counsel as needed. Functional leaders mature into VPs; Sales uses SVP and AVP leadership.

### Responsibilities and authority

- Set strategy, doctrine, risk appetite, financial controls and delegated decision limits.
- Maintain a Leadership Council consisting of the CEO, COO, Product/Technology leader, Music/Creative Operations leader and CFO/Legal participation when relevant.
- Record consequential decisions, evidence, dissent, owner, effective date, review date and exceptions.
- Require independent review for conflicts, sensitive investigations, material financial actions and exceptional access.
- Preserve escalation routes that do not depend on the person implicated.

### Boundaries and metrics

There is no universal unrestricted “leadership” permission. Access follows assignment and necessity. Executive dashboards do not substitute for direct evidence, and executive urgency does not erase rights, safety, privacy or financial controls. Measure durable outcomes, organizational health, risk handling, doctrine adherence and trustworthy execution.

## 12. Team Member Services Doctrine

### Purpose and umbrella

Team Member Services serves Funūn employees and internal Team Members. It houses HR and employment operations, Recruiting and Outreach for Team Members, internal Engagement Teams and Community, Wellness, Career Coaching and Development, and Training and Enablement. It does not recruit artists or buyers under the TMS recruiting label.

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate hiring, onboarding, records, payroll/benefit providers, leave, accommodation, performance process and exits.
- Build healthy internal engagement without compulsory social participation.
- Provide confidential wellness navigation and career development without turning coaching into discipline.
- Maintain independent complaint and escalation paths.
- Separate ordinary employee records, health/accommodation information, investigations and coaching records.
- Plan for a future VP of Team Member Services; during the first-ten stage, the COO holds accountability with qualified external providers.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure hiring quality, onboarding completion, retention patterns, internal service delivery, development access and issue resolution—not employee surveillance or forced sentiment. Protect dignity, accessibility, confidentiality and freedom from retaliation.

## 13. Training and Enablement Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Training and Enablement is a formal TMS sub-team that turns approved doctrine into usable, role-specific capability. The Playbook is the authoritative reference layer.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain company foundations, role academies, systems training and leadership development.
- Use practical exercises, simulations, supervised work and accessible alternatives.
- Require subject-matter owners to approve substantive correctness.
- Track assignment, completion, assessment, certification, expiry and refresher needs separately.
- Connect permission-sensitive work to explicit certification and delegated authority.

Training completion does not automatically equal certification, authority or system access. AI may coach, generate practice material or assist evaluation, but it cannot make the final high-impact assessment. Measure demonstrated capability, safe transfer to work, accessibility and recurring readiness—not course clicks.

## 14. Trust and Safety Doctrine

### Purpose and boundary

Trust and Safety protects people, accounts, creative work and platform integrity through fair, proportionate processes. It is not reputation protection for Funūn and cannot be used to suppress criticism or creative viewpoints.

### Responsibilities and case model

- Provide accessible reporting and emergency escalation routes.
- Keep report, case, allegation, evidence, immediate protection, investigation, decision, enforcement, appeal and retaliation monitoring as separate states.
- Apply need-to-know access, evidence preservation, conflict checks and independent review.
- Coordinate COO accountability during beta, TMS for employee matters, Product Security for technical incidents, Legal for legal matters and Finance for financial abuse.
- Use protective measures without treating them as proof of guilt.
- Provide notice, opportunity to respond, reasoned outcomes and appeal where safety and law permit.

### SLAs, metrics and ethics

Use Level 1–4 response SLAs appropriate to immediate harm and integrity risk. Measure response quality, protective effectiveness, case age, repeat harm, appeal quality and retaliation monitoring. Never reward low report counts, fast closures or severe enforcement. Refuse retaliation, favoritism and pressure to conceal material harm.

## 15. Support Operations Doctrine

### Purpose and operating model

Support owns the person's path to an answer; the specialist owns the answer. Support reports to the COO initially and may later be led by a VP of Support Operations.

### Four service lanes

- **Member and Creator Support:** account, profile, creative workflow, uploads and ordinary member navigation.
- **Client Partner and Buyer Support:** organization access, buyer workflow, licences, delivery and cue-sheet navigation.
- **Technical Support:** reproducible defects, browser/device issues, incidents, integrations and escalations to Engineering.
- **Team Member Support:** protected internal TMS and IT intake for employment and access matters.

### Workflow and first-ten ownership

Use one front door, one visible owner and preserved context so people are not forced to repeat themselves. Keep ticket, underlying problem, product defect, production occurrence and remediation as separate records. During the first-ten stage, Member Success covers member/creator support, the SVP/AE covers buyers, Product/Technology covers technical support, the COO routes work, and TMS/Trust and Safety retain secure intake for protected matters.

### Metrics and ethics

Measure time to meaningful acknowledgement, ownership, resolution quality, reopen rate, repeat-contact burden, escalation health and user understanding—not ticket closure volume. Never close work to improve a dashboard, expose restricted context during a handoff or require public disclosure of a sensitive problem.

## Shared refusal rule

Every Team Member has both the authority and responsibility to refuse or escalate work that would require them to misstate facts, exceed delegated authority, conceal a conflict, bypass rights or safety controls, misuse personal data, discriminate, retaliate, or compromise the integrity of Funūn's records.
