# Funūn Workforce and Commercial Scale Plan

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — 2026-09-07
**Purpose:** Translate Funūn's operating doctrines into a gated hiring sequence from one employee through a global 100-AE organization.

## Governing principles

- Headcount follows demonstrated workload, service quality, runway and delegated leadership capacity—not a calendar or appearance goal.
- Funūn net revenue, licensing GMV, rights-holder payouts, cash collected and gross profit remain separate measures.
- Selling capacity cannot grow faster than clearance, contracting, delivery, support, finance and catalogue capacity.
- One person may temporarily hold several functional responsibilities, but the records, authority boundaries and escalation paths remain distinct.
- Contractors do not count as employees and do not silently receive employee or business authority.
- The first dedicated AE is hired before the first AAE.

### Buyer-demand adjustment

If buyer demand develops strongly, two additional AE seats should replace lower-immediacy hires in the #21–35 range. The desired Month-35-person organization is approximately four to five AEs, including the producing SVP only where that person still carries a book. The displaced specialist seats move into the next hiring wave; they are not cancelled.

## When to hire the first dedicated AE

Forecast the need 60–90 days ahead. Hire when several signals converge:

- Approximately five qualified BDT handoffs are ready or approaching acceptance.
- Roughly 8–15 active high-touch buyer accounts require continuing ownership.
- Three to five simultaneous buyer workflows risk service quality.
- The producing SVP spends more than about 40% of time on routine account servicing.
- A credible 60–90-day starting book exists.
- Ramp-adjusted pipeline is approximately three times the proposed quota.
- Sync, Catalogue, Rights, Finance and Support can fulfill what the AE may sell.

## Ten-AE organization

At maturity, ten AEs require approximately:

- One SVP of Sales.
- One to two AVPs as span and segmentation require.
- Ten AEs.
- Approximately four to six AAEs.
- Sales Operations and Enablement capacity.
- Approximately $12.5–17.5M of AE-attributed net revenue at the planning range of $1.25–1.75M per mature AE.
- Approximately $18–25M of total annual net revenue when other recurring products are included.

Reach ten AEs only when each additional seat has a credible starting book, pipeline remains near three times ramp-adjusted quota, service SLAs remain healthy and the fulfillment functions can absorb the work.

## One-hundred-AE global organization

### Market finding

The global market can conditionally support 100 productive Funūn AEs only if Funūn becomes broader than a conventional sync-placement company.

- IFPI reported $31.7B in global recorded-music revenue for 2025 and $641M in recorded-music synchronization revenue. Recorded sync declined 2% and represented approximately 2% of the recorded market. Source: [IFPI Global Music Report 2026](https://www.ifpi.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/GMR2026_SOTI.pdf).
- Reservoir Media's 2026 Form 10-K cites a $10.8B global music-publishing industry for 2025. Source: [Reservoir Media 2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1824403/000110465926067615/rsvr-20260331x10k.htm).
- CISAC reported €12.59B in global music collections for 2024; synchronization was less than 1% of all creator collections reported across repertoires, while direct publisher licensing is not fully represented by society collections. Source: [CISAC Global Collections Report 2025](https://www.cisac.org/cisac-global-collections-report-2025).

No authoritative public source located during the 2026-09-07 review provided a clean current worldwide total combining master and composition sync without overlap. Therefore, the recorded-sync figure must not be represented as the entire combined sync market.

### Economic conclusion

A 100-AE company is not substantiated by sync commission alone. At $100M of Funūn licensing revenue and a 20% net share, Funūn would need $500M in licensing GMV—equivalent to roughly 78% of IFPI's entire recorded-sync benchmark, though the comparison is imperfect because Funūn transactions may combine master and composition rights.

The scale becomes credible only through several recurring engines:

- Paid member subscriptions.
- Client Partner subscriptions and workflow products.
- Funūn's net share of licensing transactions.
- Catalogue, metadata, data and enterprise integrations.
- Optional administration and professional services.
- Cue-sheet and production workflow infrastructure.

## Anti-gaming and capacity protections

- Do not compensate solely for contacts, pitches, activity or gross licensing value.
- Do not count rights-holder payouts as Funūn revenue or operating cash.
- Preserve relationship origin, primary ownership, supporting contribution and deal-operation credit as separate facts.
- Do not open a sales seat without a credible starting book and fulfillment capacity.
- Pause an expansion wave when rights accuracy, service, collection, reliability or member outcomes materially deteriorate.
- Do not allow revenue urgency to override clearance, consent, contracts, financial controls, verification or safety.

## Governing scale principle

> Funūn earns the right to add selling capacity by proving it can responsibly fulfill, document, collect and support the work that capacity creates.
