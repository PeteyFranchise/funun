# Funūn Organizational Doctrine Package

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — packaged 2026-09-07; not yet committed or published to The Playbook
**Audience:** Funūn Team Members, Leadership, product designers and implementation teams
**Purpose:** Define how Funūn's teams serve members and Client Partners, exercise authority, coordinate work and scale responsibly.

## Governing idea

Funūn is one coordinated organization with specialist lanes. A relationship or transaction may move through several teams, but the person being served should experience one visible owner, clear next steps and preserved context.

Technical capability does not create business authority. A title does not create unrestricted data access. Training completion does not automatically create certification, approval power or system permission.

## Shared doctrine structure

Each function is governed through the same operating questions:

- Purpose, boundary and intended outcome.
- Core and expanded responsibilities.
- Decision authority and escalation.
- Lifecycle and status architecture.
- Daily workflow and console requirements.
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- Communication, privacy and recordkeeping.
- Metrics and anti-gaming safeguards.
- Ethics, conflicts and the duty to refuse improper work.

## Shared title language

- Use **Team Lead**, not “Manager,” for internal people-leadership titles.
- Functional leadership matures toward Vice Presidents, such as VP of A&R or VP of Team Member Services.
- Sales uses the approved ladder: SVP of Sales → AVPs of Sales → Senior AEs → AEs → AAEs.
- An AAE may support an AE with a large or complex book while owning smaller accounts of their own. Supporting work receives visible attribution and the AAE receives a defined path to AE.
