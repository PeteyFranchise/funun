# Funūn Business Development Team Role and Console Doctrine

**Status:** OPEN — foundation owner-approved on 2026-09-06; expanded core responsibilities and remaining authority decisions are still under discussion
**Intended home:** The Playbook → Business Development → Role Doctrine
**Purpose:** Internal operating doctrine, onboarding reference and product authority for the Funūn BDT Team Member console
**Audience:** BDT, A&R, AE, Leadership and other authorized Funūn Team Members

## Working definition

Funūn's Business Development Team primarily develops qualified relationships with Client
Partner organizations and music buyers. BDT creates demand for the Funūn creative network,
qualifies the relationship and prepares it for durable ownership by the appropriate
receiving team.

The governing division of labor is:

> BDT opens and qualifies the buyer relationship. AEs operate and grow the account.
> A&Rs connect qualified demand to artists, songwriters and catalogue.

## Approved scope

BDT primarily develops relationships with:

- Client Partner organizations.
- Music supervisors and authorized music buyers.
- Film, television, advertising, gaming, media and production organizations seeking music.
- Agencies or representatives legitimately sourcing music for authorized clients.
- Other verified organizations that may license, commission, purchase or place music through Funūn.

BDT's purpose is to create qualified demand for the Funūn creative network.

## Ownership outside BDT

### A&R-owned relationship development

- Record labels.
- Music publishers.
- Distributors.
- Producers and production teams.
- Artist managers.
- Music service providers.
- Other creative-industry partners supporting artist and songwriter development.

### IT and Leadership-owned relationship development

- Technology partnerships.
- Platform integrations.
- Infrastructure vendors.
- Data and delivery integrations.
- Security and technical-service relationships.

A&R, AE, BDT or Marketing may identify a potential technical partner, but they route the
relationship to IT and Leadership rather than negotiating independently.

### Leadership and Marketing-owned relationship development

- Brand partnerships.
- Sponsorships.
- Corporate strategic partnerships.
- Funūn-level promotional alliances.
- Company representation and organizational positioning.

A brand seeking music for a campaign may still be a BDT-developed music buyer. A brand
seeking to sponsor Funūn or form a company-wide alliance belongs to Marketing and Leadership.

## One organization may have multiple Funūn relationships

An organization is not forced into one permanent category. Each relationship has its own
purpose, internal owner and authority boundary.

For example, a record label could simultaneously be:

- An A&R relationship for artist development.
- A Client Partner purchasing or licensing music.
- A technology integration partner.
- A strategic corporate partner.

Funūn classifies and assigns each relationship or opportunity separately.

## BDT-to-AE incubation and handoff

When BDT develops and qualifies a new Client Partner relationship, the originating BDT
Team Member remains the relationship sponsor and joint owner for approximately the first
six months.

Once an AE accepts the account:

- The AE becomes the primary day-to-day client contact immediately.
- The BDT originator preserves the history, trust, introductions and original strategic context.
- Both owners can see the relationship plan, key communications, open opportunities, commitments and handoff progress.
- Responsibilities are explicitly assigned so joint ownership does not create duplicate outreach or uncertain accountability.
- Important meetings include both owners when continuity would benefit.
- The client experiences one coordinated Funūn team.

Six months is a default rather than an inflexible deadline. The handoff may finish earlier
when the client is comfortable, the AE has sufficient context and both owners agree that
continuity is protected. It may be extended for a strategic or complex relationship, an
important active opportunity, an owner change or another documented reason approved by
Leadership.

### Full-handoff standard

The receiving AE or team accepts full ownership only when:

- The receiving owner accepts responsibility.
- Key contacts and decision-makers are documented.
- Account goals and communication preferences are understood.
- Active opportunities, promises, risks and next actions are current.
- Relevant meeting and relationship history is available.
- The client has been introduced to the long-term owner.
- No important knowledge remains solely with the BDT originator.
- The handoff date and ownership change are recorded.

Afterward, BDT retains permanent attribution as the relationship originator but no longer
carries routine ownership unless deliberately brought back into the account.

> Origin credit is permanent. Operational ownership is transferable. Relationship context
> must never disappear during the transfer.

## Client Partner verification authority

BDT members may investigate an organization, confirm contacts, document the music-buying
use case and recommend verification. They may create and develop a prospect without
granting protected access.

Final verification may be performed by:

- A senior BDT Team Member who has been explicitly granted verification authority.
- Leadership when necessary.
- A future authorized Verification function if Funūn assigns that responsibility.

Verification authority is an explicit system capability, never an inference from a job
title, profile label or seniority claim. Every approval, rejection, suspension and
restoration records the evidence, reason and accountable decision-maker.

> The relationship owner gathers the evidence. An authorized verifier grants the access.

## Verification must not obstruct a ready organic buyer

Organizational verification controls continuing Client Partner privileges. It must not
become an unnecessary gate in front of a legitimate buyer who finds Funūn organically and
is ready to complete an eligible transaction.

### Ongoing Client Partner privileges

Full verification may govern:

- A verified organization workspace.
- Broad or continuing access to The Crate.
- Organization Team Member invitations.
- Saved searches, briefs, shortlists and shared account history.
- Negotiated commercial terms or organization-level permissions.

### Transaction-ready buyer pathway

An organic buyer may license an eligible song through just-in-time checks without first
completing full Client Partner onboarding. Funūn must:

- Confirm the buyer's identity and contact information.
- Record the buyer's organization when applicable.
- Define intended use, media, territory, term and other licence parameters.
- Confirm that the exact song and recording are cleared for that licence.
- Present the exact agreement and commercial terms.
- Collect required signatures and payment.
- Run appropriate fraud, sanctions, payment and risk checks.
- Generate an immutable licence, delivery authorization and receipt.
- Offer continuing Client Partner verification after the transaction.

The transaction creates a relationship record and follow-up opportunity without
retroactively treating the buyer as a fully verified Client Partner.

> Verify the privilege at the level where it is needed. Do not require full organizational
> onboarding to complete a safe, eligible single-song transaction.

## Instant versus reviewed licences

Instant licensing is available only when the artist or authorized controller has
pre-authorized the exact:

- Song and recording version.
- Permitted type of use.
- Territory and term.
- Pricing.
- Exclusivity status.
- Delivery asset.
- Standard agreement.

Anything outside those boundaries moves into an assisted or manually reviewed licensing
path. Funūn may pause a transaction when rights or authority are incomplete or disputed;
the use requires negotiation or additional approval; identity, payment, fraud, sanctions
or safety checks fail; the request exceeds pre-authorized terms; or the buyer requests
exclusivity, custom language, unusually broad rights or other exceptional conditions.

## Console implications captured so far

The BDT/CRM system must record:

- Prospect, organic buyer, transaction-cleared buyer and verified Client Partner as distinct states.
- BDT originator, receiving owner and receiving team.
- Relationship-qualified date and joint-ownership start.
- Current primary day-to-day owner and target handoff date.
- Explicit responsibility assignments during joint ownership.
- 30-, 90- and 180-day transition reviews.
- Handoff checklist, readiness, approved extensions and completed handoff date.
- Permanent ownership and origin history.
- Verification recommendation, evidence, decision-maker, status and reason.
- Single-transaction access separately from ongoing organization access.
- Organic transactions as relationship-development and follow-up opportunities.

These are product implications, not a final screen design or completed authorization model.
