<objective>
Finish the A/B panel: a real waveform that agrees with the level-matched playback beside it, a
four-step speed control that preserves pitch, and the panel's own six "note" strings brought into
the phase's vocabulary.

Purpose: D-02's principle is that the picture matches what you are actually hearing. This panel
already balances two takes' playback volumes through `analyzePlaybackLevels`; drawing raw peaks
there would show one thing while playing another. D-17 and D-18 matter most here too, because
0.5x in the same key is how a writer judges intonation on a sung take.

Output: a level-matched waveform, a pitch-preserving speed control, and corrected copy.
</objective>

Apply the speed through `applyPlaybackShape` in three places, and all three are necessary:

  - when a step is pressed, against whichever `<audio>` elements currently exist;
  - inside every `<audio>`'s `onLoadedMetadata` handler, which already exists on both elements.

    only on press means switching sides at 0.75x silently returns to unity while the control still
    shows 0.75x;
  - immediately after a side change, once the new element has loaded.

Reset the speed to `DEFAULT_PLAYBACK_SPEED` inside `changeSide`, beside the existing level-match
reset — D-17 is explicit that nobody should open another take wondering why it drags. `setActive`
switches which side is audible without changing which take is loaded, so it does not reset.

Do not add a 2x step, a varispeed option, or a pitch toggle. D-18 is a decision, not a default:
slowing always preserves pitch, and transient smearing on drums is accepted, not a defect.
