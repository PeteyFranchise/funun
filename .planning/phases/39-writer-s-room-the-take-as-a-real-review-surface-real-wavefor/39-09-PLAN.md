<objective>
Give first-listen friction a place to go. A pin is a bookmark, not a letter: you drop it while the
take is still playing, you carry on listening, and later you come back and write real comments on
the ones that still matter. Writing a sentence is the friction being removed — so a pin is
wordless, free to drop, free to remove, and completely invisible to everyone else.

Purpose: promotion is what keeps this a stage in one flow rather than a second parallel system.
A pin becomes a comment and disappears, so a timestamp never carries two markers meaning the same
thing.

Output: a pin layer on the waveform, a two-action popover, a one-time privacy coachmark, and a
tripwire test that fails if a pin ever learns to tell the room anything.
</objective>

  <action>
Make each pin dot a real `<button>` that opens a small inline popover — not a thread, because a pin
has no thread. Use the contract's classes. It offers exactly two actions.

Turn into a comment. Pre-seed the existing comment composer at the pin's timestamp, open the
comment panel, and record which pin is being promoted. Do not create a second posting path: the
writer types and presses the existing post button, `submitComment` runs exactly as it does today,
and only on its success do you delete the pin and drop it from local state. That ordering is D-12
implemented safely — if the delete fails, the writer has their comment and a harmless private pin,
never a lost comment. Clear the promotion target if the writer cancels or navigates away without
posting.

Because promotion routes through `submitComment`, the room notification and the existing
comment-changed callback fire once, from the comment, exactly as they would for any comment. That
is correct: the comment is a communication. The pin never was, and the pin's own deletion adds
nothing to the room.

Remove pin. Deletes through the single-pin route and drops it from local state with no
confirmation dialog — a pin is free to re-drop, so a confirm step would be precisely the friction
this feature exists to remove. Close the popover on either action and on an outside press.

Do not add: an edit affordance, a rename, a note field, a resolve state, a reaction bar, a pin
count anywhere in the player chrome, or a pin entry in the carry-forward offer. A pin is scratch
about one specific performance; it stays on the take it was dropped on and costs nothing to
re-drop on the next one.

Add static-markup assertions: the popover's two action labels render when a pin is selected; the
markup contains no confirmation-dialog wording; the carry-offer block's rendered output is
unchanged by the presence of pins in the fixture.
  </action>
