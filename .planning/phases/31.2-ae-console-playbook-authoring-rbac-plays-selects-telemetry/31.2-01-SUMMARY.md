## Decisions Made
- Reproduced Phase 33's exact it-team access behavior in the migration-130 seed (grant role `'it'` only; leadership passes structurally) so day-one access is unchanged — satisfies Pitfall 6 backward-compat without a second, drifting in-code check.
- Seeded the four non-sensitive rooms with every non-leadership operational role per D-31.2-03's stated default; left the leadership room with zero grant rows since leadership access is structural, never data.
- Set the `selects_track_engagement` per-(track, viewer) abuse-cap ceiling at 5000 rows (vs. migration 117's 500-row reaction cap) — heartbeat-driven engagement rows are expected to accumulate far faster than one-shot reactions over a track's viewing lifetime, so a proportionally higher ceiling avoids false-positive blocks on legitimate repeat listening while still bounding unbounded growth.
