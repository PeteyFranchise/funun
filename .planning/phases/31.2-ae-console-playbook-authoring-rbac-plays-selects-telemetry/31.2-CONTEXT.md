# Phase 31.2: AE Console — Playbook Authoring/RBAC, Plays & Selects Telemetry - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Close the coaching loop and add measurement on top of the shipped AE Console (Phase 31.1) and the read-only Playbook shell (Phase 33). Slice 2b of Phase 31 delivers four connected capabilities:

1. **Playbook authoring** — leadership authors the three Playbook asset types (SOPs, Topics, Plays) on the Phase-33 shell.
2. **Role×room RBAC** — a data-driven permission model (rooms + per-role grants) that generalizes Phase 33's in-code room gates.
3. **Plays** — leadership composes and publishes a team "today's play"; AEs see it and act; leadership measures follow-through.
4. **Selects engagement telemetry (R13)** — capture how clients actually engage with the Selects sent to them (real audible listen-time), surfaced to the AE and leadership.

**Requirements:** R9 (Playbook / SOPs), R13 (Selects telemetry). **Depends on:** Phase 31.1 (Client Partners room, relationship health, Game Plan) + Phase 33 (Playbook shell).

**Out of scope (deferred):** full in-app social posting via the Buffer/campaign tooling; AI-generated topics/coaching questions; the Phase-32 observability/security track.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### RBAC & Access Model
- **D-31.2-01 — Full data-driven access editor.** Build the DB-stored rooms + per-room role grants + a leadership-editable **clickable room×role matrix** (the "access editor" surface). Guardrails: **last-admin protection** and an **audit log** on every grant change. This makes Phase 33's hardcoded in-code room gates (`requireStaff([...])`) data-driven/editable, and builds on the shipped roles-as-a-set model (migration 119). This is the larger RBAC build — chosen deliberately (it is the intended RBAC-phase deliverable, not a shortcut).
- **D-31.2-02 — Room-lead = both.** There is a distinct **per-room room-lead role** (approves that room's entries, manages its sub-groups) **AND** leadership holds room-lead abilities everywhere (leadership is a strict superset of room-lead). Room-leads are assigned via the Team admin surface (roles-as-data).
- **D-31.2-03 — Mixed reading.** Most Playbook rooms are **readable by all Team Members** (transparency across teams); **sensitive rooms (IT, Leadership) are view-gated** per role. Create/edit/publish always follows the authority gradient regardless of read access.
- **D-31.2-04 — Authority gradient (carried from the deliberation).** Rooms → **admins only**; sub-groups → **room-leads + admins**; entries/articles → **any member of that room drafts** → **room-lead OR leadership approves** → published.

### Playbook Authoring + Topic Sourcing
- **D-31.2-05 — Author all three asset types now.** 31.2 ships an authoring UI for **SOPs** (checklists), **Topics** (coaching bundles: a heading + open-ended questions), and **Plays** (leadership actions). R9's SOPs land alongside the coaching-loop pieces.
- **D-31.2-06 — Role-tiered publish gate.** **Leadership + room-leads publish on-save** (they are the approvers — no pointless self-approval); **regular Team Members submit a draft → room-lead/leadership approves → publishes.** Mirrors the working Tips precedent (`tip_draft` → `tip_approved`).
- **D-31.2-07 — Game-Plan topic picker keeps BOTH.** Authored Playbook **Topics augment** the 31.1 seeded starter topics in the Game-Plan picker (both appear together) — they do **not** replace the starters. The Playbook grows the menu over time; the AE always has something to pick even when the Playbook is sparse. Curated pick; **AI-generated topics stay deferred.**

### Plays & "Today's Play"
- **D-31.2-08 — One active play, many assignments.** There is **one active team-wide "today's play" at a time** (publishing a new one replaces it). A Play is a **container of several assignments** that leadership adds — structurally analogous to a Game Plan holding several topics.
- **D-31.2-09 — Two assignment kinds.**
  - **(a) Client-targeted** — the assignment targets a **health band OR a pipeline stage**, evaluated **per-AE against their OWN book**; the AE clicks it to **deep-link into their My Client Partners list filtered to those clients** (reuses the 31.1 room filters + revised health bands 🟢≤30/🟡31–60/🔴61–180/🔵>180).
  - **(b) General team task** — a **checkable directive** (title + note + optional link/attachment/content), **not** client-filtered, e.g. a **"social media play."**
- **D-31.2-10 — Social/general task: directive now, posting-hook fast-follow.** The general/social assignment **carries its content** (copy + asset + link) and is posting-ready + forward-compatible, but **actual in-app posting via the Buffer/campaign tooling is a DEFERRED fast-follow** (not in 31.2). In 31.2 the AE reads the directive and marks it done.
- **D-31.2-11 — Follow-through closes the loop.** The AE **marks assignments done** (both kinds); **leadership sees a "who's acted" completion rollup.** This is the measurement half of the phase. Banner placement: **top of My Client Partners** (the AE home).

### Selects Telemetry (R13)
- **D-31.2-12 — Full engagement signal.** Capture a **genuine audible listen-time accumulator** (play/pause/seek-aware — NOT a naive wall-clock timer), **per-track replay detail**, and **open/view events**, alongside the existing 31.1 reactions + approve/request-changes.
- **D-31.2-13 — Surfaces in two places.** An **AE per-Selects view** (how each client engaged with what the AE sent) **AND** a **leadership aggregate rollup** across the team's book (who's getting listens, which Selects land).
- **D-31.2-14 — Per-recipient attribution, staff-only.** Engagement is recorded against the **recipient the Selects was sent to** ("this client org listened to track X for Y seconds"). Standard B2B sales-tool posture (like email open-tracking). **Internal/staff-only — NEVER shown to the client.**

### Claude's Discretion
- The **audible-time accumulator's technical approach** (event model, debounce, seek/pause handling, client vs server aggregation, storage schema) is left to research + planning — the phase watch-out ("genuine accumulator, not a naive timer") is the only constraint.
- **Exact schema, table names, and admin-tier naming** for the access-editor rooms/grants + room-lead assignment UI mechanics are implementation details for the planner, consistent with the existing `funun_staff` roles model + migration doctrine (zero-policy + REVOKE, human-gated push).

</decisions>

- Leadership-only server gating with **hide-not-filter** (`requireStaff(['leadership'])`, machine-verified in 31.1).

- New tables ship **RLS zero-policy + REVOKE from authenticated/anon**, staff service-role routes only, human-gated `supabase db push` (migration doctrine from 128/129).
