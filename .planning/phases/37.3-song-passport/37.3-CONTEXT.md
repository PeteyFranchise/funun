# Phase 37.3 — Song Passport Context

**Execution status (2026-09-02):** Slices 1–7 are code-complete behind server-owned rollout
controls and migrations 150–156 are applied. The feature remains disabled until deployment
plus pilot/global activation and UAT.

## Phase outcome

A song carries one permissioned, versioned source of truth from the Writer's Room through
recording versions and into the Release Report without duplicate entry, silent overwrite,
privacy leakage or source-audio mutation.

## Locked inputs

- `.planning/deliberations/song-passport-doctrine.md` is the doctrine authority.
- One Passport belongs to one underlying work.
- Contributor, composition, recording-version and release layers remain separate.
- Inherited, draft, confirmed, locked, outdated and disputed are distinct states.
- Confirmation and approval are separate actions.
- Creative collaboration never grants legal, ownership, release or delivery authority.
- Tasks route responsibility; qualifying facts and approvals determine readiness.
- Original audio is immutable; metadata artifacts are generated copies or sidecars.
- DDEX standards implementation remains Phase 37.4.
- Partner-validated delivery remains Phase 37.5.

## Seven implementation mini-phases

1. Architecture and additive schema foundation
2. Legacy discovery, backfill and reconciliation
3. Passport UI inside Sound Vault
4. Confirmation, approval, permissions and conflict workflows
5. Recording versions, master selection and Release Report graduation
6. Snapshots, delivery artifacts, portability and custody history
7. Pilot rollout, observability, support and claims review

## User-visible north star

An artist opens a song and can answer, without searching multiple rooms:

- Who contributed and in what role?
- Who confirmed each important fact?
- Which recording version is being discussed or released?
- Which splits, agreements and identifiers govern this use?
- What is missing, outdated, disputed or waiting on someone?
- What exact information and file were exported or delivered?

## Non-goals

- A Google Docs-style character-merging engine
- Replacing Split Sheets or Contract Locker with editable Passport fields
- Treating metadata readiness as legal clearance
- Mutating source audio to keep tags current
- Claiming direct distributor/DSP/society delivery before partner acceptance
- Letting AI confirm identity, rights, ownership or authority

## Definition of done

One owner and two contributors create a work with three recording versions. Approved
identity and composition facts flow into every version; each version keeps independent
recording credits. A final master is selected and graduated into a release without re-entry.
The release adds only release-layer facts. Tagged MP3 and sidecar artifacts derive from one
immutable snapshot, no private data leaks, the source hash stays unchanged, and subsequent
profile edits cannot rewrite the approved or delivered record.

The pilot must also prove conflict handling, disconnected/retried writes, least-authority
access, snapshot stability, custody-transfer history and rollback to the pre-Passport UI
without data loss.
