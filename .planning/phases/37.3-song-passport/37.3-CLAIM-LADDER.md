# Song Passport Evidence-Scoped Claim Ladder

## Built behind activation controls

Funūn keeps versioned contributor, composition, recording and release facts with source/trust states; supports scoped proposals, self-confirmation, disputes and immutable approvals; links an exact master to a Release Report; and generates snapshot-bound JSON, sidecar and eligible tagged-MP3 copies with hashes and generation receipts.

## Coming after production pilot acceptance

Public availability of those same capabilities after migrations, deployment, cohort UAT, support readiness and zero stop-ship incidents. Do not call feature-gated code generally live.

## Roadmap / partner exploration

Validated CWR/RIN/ERN messages, DPID/licence operations, named distributor/DSP/society APIs, direct transmission, recipient acknowledgments, correction/takedown flows and broader container embedding.

## Never imply from this phase

- DDEX certification or DDEX identity embedded in a song
- Automatic ownership adjudication or legal advice
- Universal zero-entry distributor registration
- Certified direct delivery to Secretly, a distributor, DSP or society
- That a generation receipt proves download, transmission, acceptance, licensing or payment
