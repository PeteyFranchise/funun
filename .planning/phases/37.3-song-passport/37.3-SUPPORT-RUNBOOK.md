# Song Passport Support Runbook

## First response

Collect work ID, Passport ID, action/artifact ID, approximate timestamp, account ID and visible error. Do not ask for lyrics, legal names, split percentages, contracts, addresses, master files or recipient emails in a normal ticket.

## Triage

- Authorization/privacy/source mutation/silent overwrite: high or critical; stop cohort growth.
- Export/graduation/custody evidence mismatch: high; disable affected writes/exports.
- Availability or ordinary validation error: medium/low unless repeated across accounts.

## Recovery rules

- Never edit/delete ledger values, snapshots, actions, master designations, release links, artifacts or custody events to hide a ticket.
- Correct facts with successor revisions/approvals; correct delivery/custody evidence with successor events.
- Preserve failed-action IDs and Sentry/server evidence. Use the emergency switch before broad repair.

Engineering owns authorization/source integrity/transactions. Operations owns cohorts/claims. Legal reviews transfer instruments, rights data and deletion holds. Funūn does not adjudicate ownership or act as counsel.
