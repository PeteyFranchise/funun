<objective>
Close the two pure-logic halves of R-08: `isLiveQualifyingEvidence` (`lib/workspaces/evidence.ts`)
checks `declaredScope`, `expiresAt` and `supersededAt` but **never reads `effectiveFrom`**, so an
agreement uploaded today and effective next month is treated as live immediately (WSR-15); and
there is no confirmation concept anywhere in the module, so a workspace admin's own upload becomes
authority-tier evidence the instant the resolver next runs (WSR-14, finding F8).

Purpose: D-36 says the rights holder declares the scope and the document is supporting evidence for
that declaration. The workspace may draft; only the subject Member's confirmation activates it.
This plan makes the compute-on-read ladder enforce that, and updates the one I/O caller that feeds
it so the phase stays type-clean at every commit.

Output: an evidence ladder that gates on time, confirmation and document presence, plus the
`loadRelationshipTier` select that supplies those three facts.
</objective>
