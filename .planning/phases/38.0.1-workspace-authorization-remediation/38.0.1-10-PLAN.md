<objective>
R-02's core: remove the workspace branch from the four child tables entirely, and replace it with
column-allowlist SECURITY DEFINER read functions.

**The verified defect (F2/F3).** Migration 186 added
`OR (SELECT public.workspace_project_permission(project_id, auth.uid(), 'view_summaries'))` to the
SELECT policy and `... 'edit_metadata'` to the write policy on each of `tracks`, `vault_assets`,
`vault_documents` and `tool_outputs`. RLS grants WHOLE ROWS: `view_summaries` therefore hands over
every column of those tables, and `edit_metadata` maps onto `FOR ALL` policies, which is
delete-and-overwrite authority. A permission catalogue that names nineteen granular capabilities is
being enforced by a policy that knows two.

**The fix.** A view cannot vary its exposed columns per requested permission, and PostgREST's
`select=` mechanism cannot either. A function's `RETURNS TABLE` clause IS the contract, and a caller
cannot ask for a column outside it. That is the whole reason R-02 chose functions.

Purpose: after this migration, `vault_projects` is the only table with a direct workspace RLS branch.

Output: migration 193 and the extended negative suite. Authored only; pushed at plan 11's joint
checkpoint with 191/192/194.
</objective>
