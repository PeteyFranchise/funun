<objective>
The schema half of R-01 (consent root + delegation lineage), R-08's confirmation state, and R-16's
NOT NULL tightening.

**Why the NOT NULL belongs here and not in the hygiene bucket:** migration 186's helper joins
`workspace_roster_relationships` with `(a.relationship_id IS NULL OR r.id = a.relationship_id)`.
When an attachment's `relationship_id` is NULL, that clause matches ANY accepted relationship in the
workspace. Migration 194 then binds custody with `p.user_id = r.member_user_id` — against whichever
relationship happened to join. A nullable `relationship_id` therefore defeats WSR-06's custody
binding directly. R-16 is load-bearing for slice B, not cosmetic.

Purpose: give plans 06, 07, 08 and 09 the columns they need, in one additive migration, gated on the
production pre-flight.

Output: migration 193 and its text-lock test. Authored only; the push happens at plan 11's joint
checkpoint alongside 190/191/192, because 190's helper depends on this schema existing.
</objective>
