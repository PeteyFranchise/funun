<objective>
Build the layered-tagging foundation: close the missing INSTRUMENT controlled-vocabulary gap, extend the track descriptors shape so AI suggestions and staff refinements are provenance-tracked alongside (never on top of) the artist-confirmed values, and add the pure AI-suggest + merge modules.

Purpose: Tagging (mood/genre/energy/instrument) is what the Crate filters, search, and brief-matching run on. This plan makes AI suggestions safe (vocab-constrained, non-destructive) and gives instruments a real shared vocab so AI-suggested instruments are filter-visible rather than free-text.
Output: extended `lib/metadata/schema.ts`, new `lib/tagging/ai-tag.ts`, new `lib/tagging/tag-merge.ts`.
</objective>
