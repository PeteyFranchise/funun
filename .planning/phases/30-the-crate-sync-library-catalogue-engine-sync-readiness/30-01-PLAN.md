<objective>
Build the two pure modules the whole Sync Library engine composes: a per-track Sync Readiness derivation that reuses the Wave 1 readiness engine, and the inclusion-gate predicate that decides whether a track is clear to admit or must enter the completion pipeline.

Output: `lib/sync-library/readiness.ts` and `lib/sync-library/gate.ts`.
</objective>
