Ship the two server write-paths that make layered tagging real: an AI tag-suggest route (staff-triggered, provenance-preserving) and a staff tag-refine route (staff curates the confirmed tags). Artist confirm already exists via MetadataStudio's descriptor fields; this plan adds the AI + staff layers.

Purpose: Tagging drives Crate filters, search, and brief-matching. This plan lets staff generate AI suggestions and curate tags for consistency — without ever clobbering what the artist authored.
Output: new `app/api/sync-library/tag-suggest/route.ts`, new `app/api/sync-library/tag-refine/route.ts`.
</objective>
