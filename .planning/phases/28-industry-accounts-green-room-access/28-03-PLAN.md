<objective>
Retire the legacy `role='curator'` account by repointing its single mint site. `app/api/curators/claim/[token]/
route.ts` is the ONLY writer of `admin.createUser({ app_metadata: { role: 'curator' } })` in the codebase
(RESEARCH / PATTERNS confirmed). A claimed curator directory contact should become a real Industry account
(`member_type='industry'`, badge `playlist_curator`) — with the Green Room + opportunity-posting tools — not the
strictly-weaker legacy curator account (INDUSTRY-04).

To avoid RESEARCH Pitfall 4 (double-send / wrong-copy email) and the "don't call the higher-level function
wholesale" anti-pattern, this plan extracts a small reusable primitive `provisionIndustryAccount()` from
`createIndustryMember()` — account creation ONLY (the atomic `admin.createUser` with `app_metadata.role='industry'`
+ `role_badges`/`profile_roles` in user_metadata), no email. `createIndustryMember()` keeps its cold-invite email;
the claim route calls the primitive then sends its own curator-claim welcome copy. This same primitive is the
enabling substrate for a future community/Team-Member industry invite (INDUSTRY-03) — see the note below.

Scope guardrails (locked): the `handle_new_user()` curator branch and the `(curator-portal)` route group are NOT
deleted here (RESEARCH Anti-Pattern: two-step retirement). Repointing the mint site stops NEW `role='curator'`
accounts; the physical removal of the dead branch/route-group is a documented follow-up, gated on the live-DB
count confirmed at Plan 28-05's checkpoint. The `curators` table schema is untouched.

INDUSTRY-03 status (planner call, documented): the invite MECHANISM is resolved — token-claim self-serve reusing
`provisionIndustryAccount()` + the existing `curators.claim_token` precedent. The actual non-admin invite
route/UI is NOT built this phase (owner-vision, and the trust model — who may invite, admin-review vs inviter
vouching — is an unresolved product decision RESEARCH flags for discuss-phase). This plan lands the enabling
primitive only; the route is forward-scoped and surfaced to the owner.

Purpose: a claimed curator gets a full Industry account; no more legacy curator accounts are minted.
Output: `provisionIndustryAccount()` + a repointed, single-email claim route.
</objective>
