<objective>
Build the hygiene logic: the three pure modules that decide what Funūn says to an artist about
ownership, splits, and what to do next.

`lib/catalogue/ai-entries.ts` composes the citation and the four-line receipt, and enforces the
when-in-doubt rule structurally. `lib/catalogue/splits.ts` turns "this person is a writer" into
an equal-share redraft of the living sheet using the split-sheet machinery that already exists.
`lib/catalogue/guiding-line.ts` picks the song's single next-best step and applies the nudge
cadence.

Purpose: this is where the doctrine either holds or leaks. The when-in-doubt rule is the
difference between a citation that is a badge and a citation that is a false record — and the
only honest way to enforce it is to make the safe label unreachable when no human take exists.
The equal-split default and the people-not-numbers rule are locked owner decisions that a
component must never get to reinterpret. And the guiding line is one sentence by design; a
resolver that can return two has already broken sketch 005-C.

Output: three pure modules and three Jest suites, green with no DB connection.
</objective>

| work membership → split-sheet ownership | Two different facts that a single careless call would conflate |
