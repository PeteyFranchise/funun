<objective>
Build the lyrics pad exactly as sketch 006-A decided it.

Implements: `.claude/skills/sketch-findings-funun/sources/006-lyrics-pad/variant-a.html`, with the
rules recorded in `.claude/skills/sketch-findings-funun/references/catalogue-hygiene-ui.md` —
INSERT-ANYWHERE, REPEAT, RENUMBERING, PERFORMER and DEFAULT-PERFORMER.

`LyricBlockCard.tsx` is the section card: grip, uppercase indigo section label, author avatar,
singer cluster, and the lyric body. `CopyLyricMenu.tsx` is the two-flavour export. `LyricsPad.tsx`
is the sortable container with the header, the add-section chip row and the insert-anywhere
dividers.

Purpose: the pad is the second half of what an artist owns. The hum evidences the melody, the pad
evidences the words. Attribution is a property of the text — one author badge per block — which is
what makes section-level diary entries possible, what tells the splits nudge who to name, and what
answers a dispute with the block itself rather than with somebody's memory.

Output: three components and three `renderToStaticMarkup` suites.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| collaborator-entered lyric text → rendered pad | Free text from another account renders inside the owner's page |
| clipboard export → an artist's evidence | What is copied is what the artist may later rely on |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-37-49 | Tampering | XSS through collaborator-entered lyric or custom-label text | medium | mitigate | React's default JSX escaping covers every rendered string, and no component in this plan uses raw-HTML injection. The prohibition is stated at plan level so a later "render markdown in lyrics" change has to justify itself. |
| T-37-50 | Tampering | Displayed lyric diverging from the exported lyric | medium | mitigate | Both the on-screen resolution and the export come from the same `lib/catalogue/blocks.ts` functions; the components compute neither. |
| T-37-51 | Repudiation | An export that hides a repeat and understates the song | low | mitigate | `serializeLyrics()` expands every repeat in full for both flavours, and plan 02's suite asserts it. |
| T-37-52 | Tampering | A lost or silently reverted reorder under concurrent editing | medium | mitigate | The drag-end handler surfaces the route's 409 and reverts to the server order rather than retrying blindly on top of a changed set. |
| T-37-53 | Information Disclosure | Clipboard failure leaving the artist without their own words | low | mitigate | A refused or unavailable clipboard API degrades to a selectable text area rather than a no-op. |
| T-37-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | No package-manager install task exists in this plan — `@dnd-kit/core`, `@dnd-kit/sortable` and `@dnd-kit/utilities` are already dependencies in active use. |
