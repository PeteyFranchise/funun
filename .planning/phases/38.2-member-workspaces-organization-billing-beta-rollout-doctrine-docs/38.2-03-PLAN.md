# Plan 38.2-03 — Workspace Rollout and Emergency Operations Console

## Objective

Expose the already-shipped D-55 cohort boundary and D-56 platform kill switch through one
leadership-only operational console, without creating a second source of truth or a second stop
button.

## Scope

1. Reuse `workspace_access_config`, `workspace_cohorts`, and
   `workspace_access_permitted`; do not introduce duplicate rollout tables.
2. Add leadership-only reads and narrowly validated cohort membership mutations.
3. Add an admin console showing platform status, general/pilot environment posture, cohort members,
   and workspace billing states.
4. Preserve the current kill-switch route's ability to operate while workspace access is disabled.
5. Add audit records for every control mutation and never expose cohort membership to Members.

## Validation

- Staff-role authorization, exact-input, rate-limit, and audit tests.
- Source invariants proving one kill switch and one cohort table remain authoritative.
- TypeScript, lint, production build, and deferred leadership UAT.

## Risks and controls

- Only Leadership may operate the platform-wide switch or beta cohort.
- Non-cohort Members continue receiving 404, not 403.
- Environment flags may end the cohort requirement but cannot override a disabled database switch.
