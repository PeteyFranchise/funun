# Plan 38.2-04 — Account Architecture and Workspace Doctrine Publication

## Objective

Make the workspace model legible to future builders and Funūn Team Members: workspaces are operating
contexts under a Member identity, never a fourth account class and never evidence of rights,
credits, royalties, signature authority, or ownership.

## Scope

1. Update `docs/architecture/ACCOUNT-TYPES.md` with the approved workspace, custody, authority,
   payout/tax exclusion, split-sheet, switching, and multi-organization rules.
2. Add a company-wide Playbook doctrine plus focused A&R/Sales and IT operating guidance.
3. Package Playbook publication in owner-gated candidate migration 223.
4. Add migration and manifest tests so doctrine content cannot silently drift from the approved
   architecture.
5. Record migration gates and human publication/UAT steps.

## Validation

- Documentation consistency and migration source tests.
- Full automated suite, TypeScript, lint, and production build.
- Candidate migration remains unapplied and unpublished until owner approval.

## Risks and controls

- Documentation must not imply that workspace membership creates ownership, credit, splits, or
  payment rights.
- Billing descriptions stay beta-specific and make no public pricing promise.
- Existing Member, Client Partner, and Funūn Team Member identity classes remain unchanged.
