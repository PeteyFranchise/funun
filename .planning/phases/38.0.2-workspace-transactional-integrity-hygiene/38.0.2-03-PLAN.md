<objective>
Add the two role predicates the rest of the phase gates on: owner-only owner management (R-05 /
WSR-07) and the guest role floor on project access (R-20 / WSR-29).

R-05 says only owners may promote to owner and admins may not edit or remove owner rows.
`canManageWorkspaceMembers` returns true for owner **and** admin and is what `PATCH`/`DELETE` on
`/members` gate on today. It must not be widened and must not be narrowed — narrowing it would take
ordinary member management away from admins, which no decision asks for. So this plan adds a
**separate** owner-only predicate beside it.

R-20 adds a role floor excluding `guest` from project access. Its primary home is migration 197's
`workspace_project_permission` hop 2 (plan 09). This plan supplies the API-layer twin, because this
repo's doctrine is two independent layers agreeing — migrations 078, 136, 187, 190, 196 — and
WSR-17 exists precisely because those two layers once disagreed about `expires_at`.

**How the API-side floor is applied, stated precisely so it is not over-applied.** R-20 says guests
keep everything that is NOT project data: workspace membership, chrome, and whatever R-12's
proposal rules allow. So the floor must NOT be added to `requireWorkspaceAccess` itself, which
gates every route under `/api/workspaces/**` including the chrome ones. It is a named predicate
here, composed through the existing `requireWorkspaceRole` at the routes that actually reach a
Member's project data. Plan 13 does that wiring and names the exact routes.

Purpose: give plans 09, 12 and 13 one settled predicate each to build on.

Output: two new exports and their tests. No route change, no migration.

</objective>
