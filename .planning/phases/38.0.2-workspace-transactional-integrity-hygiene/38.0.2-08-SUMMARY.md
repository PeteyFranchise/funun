# Phase 38.0.2 Plan 08: Ownership and Member-Transition RPCs Summary

Three transactional RPCs appended to migration 198 — atomic member role/status change with
the owner floor counted inside the write transaction, and the two sides of the ownership
transfer — plus 29 new text-lock assertions, including the source-offset check that stands
between promote-before-demote and a production `42501`.

**No database connection of any kind was opened and no migration was applied.** No
`supabase db push`, `db reset`, `migration up` or `db query` was run, not even against a
scratch database. The D-56 kill switch was not touched. The file remains deliberately
incomplete; plans 10 and 11 append sections (f) through (h), and plan 11 closes it.

## What shipped

### Section (c) — `public.workspace_change_member_role_or_status` (WSR-11 / WSR-07 / F15)

Locks rank 1 `workspaces` then rank 2 the target `workspace_members` row, both
`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`. Revalidates after the lock: kill switch, row existence, the actor's
authority **re-derived from the database** (there is no `p_actor_role` parameter and the
suite now locks the whole signature against one appearing), owner/admin, admins-may-not-
touch-owner-rows, no promotion to owner outside the transfer, no self-role-change, the
compare-and-set tokens, transition legality, and the owner floor.

The floor count is the point: it now happens **after the lock and inside the write
transaction**, which is exactly what F15's cross-transaction count was not.
`guard_workspace_never_zero_owners` becomes the backstop rather than the primary control,
and the route gets a `'floor'` outcome code instead of an English sentence to string-match.

### Sections (d) and (e) — the two-sided transfer (WSR-08 / R-22)

`workspace_nominate_owner` locks rank 1, then **both rank-2 seats in one statement with
`ORDER BY m.id`**. That ordering is not cosmetic: two nominations in opposite pairings would
otherwise take the same two rows in opposite orders. One statement locking two rows is
correct here and is commented as such — the one-row-per-statement rule governs `UPDATE`s,
whose `BEFORE ROW` triggers cannot see their own command's changes, and a locking `SELECT`
fires no trigger.

`workspace_respond_ownership_nomination` reads the transfer **unlocked** first — the only
way to learn which rank-1 and rank-2 rows to acquire before acquiring them in ascending
order — then locks 1, 2, 7 and re-decides every precondition against the locked row. The
nominator may never accept, whatever else is true.

The accept path mutates in exactly three statements: **promote the successor, then demote
the nominator, then resolve the transfer.** The rule is written at the statement and
machine-checked by source offset. Ownership **transfers** (R-22): the nominator ends as
`admin`. No add-a-second-owner RPC was built.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] Added the `'successor_no_longer_a_member'` refusal**
- **Found during:** Task 2, writing the accept path
- **Issue:** The plan specifies re-checking that the *nominator* still holds a live owner
  seat, but not the symmetric check on the *successor*. If the successor's seat was
  removed, suspended or expired between nomination and acceptance, statement 1 would match
  **zero** rows while statement 2 still matched one — demoting the only owner and leaving
  the workspace with none. That surfaces as a `42501` the person cannot act on, which is
  the floor trigger doing its job at the cost of a transaction that should never have been
  attempted.
- **Fix:** An audited refusal with its own outcome code, checked before any mutation.
- **Commit:** `6d2eead8`
- **Plan 12 must map this outcome** — it is one code beyond the vocabulary the plan listed.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] The successor's promotion audit row recorded a false `before` value**
- **Found during:** Final review of Task 2
- **Issue:** I had hardcoded `'before', 'member'`. A successor may hold `admin`,
  `contractor` or `guest`, so for three of the four non-owner roles the audit trail would
  assert something untrue about a role change. In a phase whose whole purpose is a
  dependable audit trail, a record nobody can trust is worse than no record.
- **Fix:** Capture `m.role` alongside `m.id` in the locked read that already runs; no extra
  query.
- **Commit:** `b80646ec`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Two precision gaps in plan 06's shared harness**
- **Found during:** Task 1, first suite run (2 failures)
- **Issue A:** `lockSites()` scanned `masked`, which strips `--` comment lines but keeps
  `COMMENT ON FUNCTION` statements. Section (c)'s comment correctly says *"FOR NO KEY UPDATE
  and not FOR UPDATE per LO-2"*, and LO-2 read that documentation prose as two real
  `FOR UPDATE` clauses — reporting a violation on a file whose every locking clause is
  `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`.
- **Issue B:** The schema-qualification scan read the `FROM` of the null-safe comparison
  operator `IS DISTINCT FROM` as a clause, reporting `v_member.role` and `p_expected_role`
  as unqualified relations.
- **Fix:** A line-count-preserving `maskedExecutable` view for `lockSites()`, and a
  `DISTINCT_FROM` strip alongside the `LOCK_CLAUSE` strip the scan already performed for the
  identical reason. **Neither weakens what the assertions catch** — both were proved still
  failable by mutation (cycles 1 and 2 below), and the LO-2 failure still reports the correct
  line number.
- **Why the harness rather than the SQL:** both gaps are latent for plans 10 and 11 too. Any
  `COMMENT ON FUNCTION` quoting a lock mode, and any `IS DISTINCT FROM`, would hit them.
- **Commit:** `598d9af3`

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] My own new F15 assertion was weaker than its name**
- **Found during:** Mutation cycle 8
- **Issue:** `indexOf('public.workspace_members')` matched the `%ROWTYPE` declaration in
  `DECLARE`, so searching forward for the lock clause found the **rank-1 `workspaces`** lock
  rather than the members lock. The assertion passed for the wrong reason.
- **Fix:** Anchored on `FROM public.workspace_members m` — the locked `SELECT`'s own `FROM`
  clause. Commented so it is not loosened back.
- **Commit:** `eeb46a64`

**5. [Rule 2] Reworded a comment so it cannot arm LO-2's justification token**
- **Found during:** Mutation cycle 1 preparation
- **Issue:** LO-2 treats the literal phrase `key column` in the ten comment lines above a
  locking clause as a **justification** for a stronger mode. My explanation of why the
  stronger mode is *not* needed wrapped to "...changes a key\n column...", which would have
  silently pre-authorised a future `FOR UPDATE` at that exact site.
- **Fix:** Reworded, with a comment stating why the shorter wording must not be restored.
- **Commit:** `598d9af3`

**6. [Deliberate, per plan] Kill switch RAISEs rather than returning `'disabled'`**
- The plan specifies this for section (c) and I applied it to (d) and (e) for consistency.
  It deviates from `workspace_create`'s outcome code, so it carries a justifying comment at
  the point of deviation as section (a) requires: there is nothing to audit about a globally
  disabled feature, and no audit row exists yet for a RAISE to roll back.

## Notes for later plans

**Plan 12 — the outcome vocabularies to map:**

- `workspace_change_member_role_or_status`: `ok`, `not_found`, `forbidden`,
  `forbidden_owner_row`, `promotion_requires_transfer`, `no_self_role_change`, `stale`,
  `illegal_transition`, `floor`
- `workspace_nominate_owner`: `ok`, `forbidden`, `no_self_nomination`,
  `successor_not_a_member`, `already_owner`, `nomination_open`
- `workspace_respond_ownership_nomination`: `ok`, `not_found`, `stale`, `already_resolved`,
  `forbidden`, `nominator_no_longer_owner`, **`successor_no_longer_a_member`** ← added here,
  not in the plan's list

`'floor'` is what replaces `isOwnerFloorTriggerError`'s match on the sentence *"at least one
active owner"*. Gate owner paths on `canManageOwners` **beside** `canManageWorkspaceMembers`,
not instead of it.

**Plans 10 and 11 — two harness affordances now exist for you:** `maskedExecutable` means you
may quote `FOR UPDATE` freely in a `COMMENT ON FUNCTION`, and `IS DISTINCT FROM` no longer
trips the schema-qualification scan. Also inherited from plan 06: `rpcFunctionNames()` excludes
`RETURNS TRIGGER`, and the no-`DROP TRIGGER` rule permits the house `DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS`
idempotency idiom.

**A caution for anyone editing section (c)'s comments:** LO-2's justification check is a
substring match for `key column` (or `DELETE`) in the ten comment lines above a locking
clause. Prose explaining why a stronger mode is *unnecessary* must avoid those tokens, or it
pre-authorises the thing it was arguing against.
