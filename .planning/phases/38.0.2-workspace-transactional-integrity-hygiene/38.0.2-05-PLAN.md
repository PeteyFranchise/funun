**The write-order rule this trigger family imposes, stated here because plan 08 depends on it.**
`guard_workspace_never_zero_owners` runs inside your transaction and sees your own uncommitted
writes from *previous statements*. So a two-sided ownership transfer must **promote the successor in
statement 1 and demote the incumbent in statement 2**. In that order the trigger firing on the
demotion counts the freshly-promoted successor and passes. In the reverse order it counts zero and
raises `42501`. Plan 08 writes that order; this plan writes the trigger that makes it matter.
