<objective>
Append the invitation-redemption and roster-transition RPCs to migration 198 — the transactional
close of F11 (WSR-10) and F16 (WSR-12), plus R-24's cohort gate on the acceptor (WSR-16).

**F11 — invitation redemption is four transactions.** Lookup; update the invitation to `accepted`
with a CAS on `status = 'pending'` **whose result is never checked** (the error variable catches an
error, not "zero rows matched"); seat lookup; then seat update **or** seat insert. Two concurrent
redemptions can both pass the seat lookup and both take the insert branch — the partial unique index
saves correctness with a `23505` that the route reports as a generic 500.

**F16 — roster transitions have no CAS at all.** Read the row; `assertCanTransition(row.state, ...)`;
then `.update({state}).eq('id', row.id)` with no `.eq('state', row.state)` anywhere. Two concurrent
PATCHes can both read `proposed` and one can overwrite the other's terminal state. The `block`
branch additionally upserts `workspace_roster_blocks` in a separate transaction — the "side effects"
half of WSR-12 — so a crash between the two leaves a `blocked` relationship with no block row, and
`assertCanPropose` would then permit a re-proposal.

**R-24 — the cohort gate applies to the acceptor.** If it applied only to workspace creation, one
cohort owner could pull in unlimited non-cohort Members and the pilot bound would stop meaning
anything. `app/api/workspaces/invitations/accept/route.ts` is the third kill-switch call site, and
missing it reproduces the exact shape of hotfix F7.
