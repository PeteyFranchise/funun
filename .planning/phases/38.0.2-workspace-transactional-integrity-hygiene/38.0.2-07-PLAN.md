<objective>
Make the workspace audit trail genuinely append-only, genuinely free of restricted PII, and
genuinely non-bypassable — the three halves of S1/WSR-26, R-13/WSR-19 and R-06/WSR-13.

**S1's "verify at runtime" half is already done, and the answer was the bad one.** Production check
A10 on 2026-09-07 confirmed `service_role` holds **TRUNCATE, DELETE and UPDATE** on
`workspace_audit_log`. Migration 182's `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE ... FROM PUBLIC` removed nothing from
it, because Supabase's bootstrap grants those privileges to `service_role` **directly**, and a
revoke from `PUBLIC` never touches a direct grant. The audit log is not append-only today.

**And CONTEXT.md's remedy is half wrong, deliberately recorded rather than quietly fixed.** S1 says
"revoke ... and add a rejecting policy." The revoke works. **The policy does nothing at all against
`service_role`, which carries `BYPASSRLS`** — no RLS policy, permissive or restrictive, constrains a
BYPASSRLS role. Only table privileges and **triggers** bind it, and `TRUNCATE` needs its own
statement-level trigger because row-level triggers do not fire for it.

**R-06 also does not guarantee what it sounds like.** Putting the audit INSERT inside the RPC makes
the audit non-*partial*. It does not make it non-*bypassable*: a future RPC author who simply omits
the INSERT is not caught by anything. That is F14 one layer up. A deferred constraint trigger,
checked at COMMIT, is what closes it.

Purpose: end this plan with an audit trail that an application role cannot rewrite, cannot truncate,
cannot fill with someone's email address, and cannot skip.

Output: four more sections appended to migration 197, and the matching describe blocks in its suite.
