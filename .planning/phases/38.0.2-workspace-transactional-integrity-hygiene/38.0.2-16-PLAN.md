<objective>
Move the custody-transfer response path onto its transactional RPC — the route half of WSR-09.

**F9's residual window, precisely.** The PATCH handler runs three separate transactions: read the
transfer; CAS-update the diary to `accepted` with `.eq('state','offered')`; then call
`transfer_vault_project_custody`. A crash or a concurrent transfer between steps 2 and 3 leaves the
diary saying `accepted` while custody never moved — split-brain, on the one field that decides who
can reach a Member's record.

The P0 hotfix already shipped the cheap half — the stale-custodian double filter and the single-row
check — which closed the dangerous overwrite. What is left is the window between the diary write and
the custody write, and only one transaction closes it. **WSR-09 has been deferred twice; it is not
deferred again.**

**What is kept, and why it is not duplication.** `assertMayOffer` and `assertMayRespond` are
documented in this file's own header as the second enforcement point beside migration 187's trigger,
and `assertMayRespond`'s self-dealing check is what the F1 hotfix added after a workspace admin could
once both offer and accept. They stay verbatim. `isLegalTransferTransition` stays — a pure state
machine naming the illegal edge in a sentence a `RAISE` cannot phrase. `describeTransferEffect` stays
— it is the sentence both parties see, and D-28's whole point.

**What is deleted** is the mutation sequence: the diary CAS, the `transfer_vault_project_custody`
call with its two-branch result handling, and both `logWorkspaceAction` calls on the response paths.
Keep every predicate; delete every mutation sequence.

Output: the PATCH handler rewired, with the route's first test suite.

**Non-negotiable constraints for this plan:**
- Do NOT open a database connection. The RPC is authored but **unapplied**; the tests mock the
  client.
- Do NOT flip the D-56 kill switch, and do NOT add a kill-switch consultation to this route — its
  absence is deliberate and documented (F1/F7).
- Do NOT touch anything under `lib/playbook/`, `components/playbook/`, or `app/**/playbook/`.
- Stage by explicit path only. **Never `git add -A`.**
- Use `npx tsc --noEmit`, never `npm run build`. `tsc` does not cover `next build`'s lint step —
  leave no unused import, and never add an eslint-disable naming a `@typescript-eslint/*` rule.
</objective>
