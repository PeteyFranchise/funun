# Plan 45.2-01: Recording value, consent, custody, cost, and go/no-go

## Objective

Produce an owner/counsel decision brief. Do not enable a recording permission, SDK option, schema, or UI.

## Research

1. Measure post-pilot demand and concrete use cases: remember a writing session, capture a producer demo,
   generate a recap, preserve a decision, or review later. Compare safer alternatives such as deliberate
   short clips, chat/Studio Notes, take upload, or timestamp bookmarks.
2. Obtain counsel/privacy analysis for participant consent and withdrawal across launch jurisdictions,
   minors, employees/contractors, copyright/performance, discoverability, and data-subject rights.
3. Compare provider track/composite recording, storage/encryption/regions, webhook integrity, retention/
   deletion/export, access/custody, screen-share scope, download control, and breach/incident behavior.
4. Forecast call + recording + composition + storage + egress at measured usage; define who pays, quotas,
   max duration, auto-stop, cost alerts, and deletion savings.
5. Prototype only in an isolated non-production room using synthetic content if counsel permits. Verify all-
   participant indicators, consent state, late join, withdrawal, screen-share change, failure, and deletion.
6. Return no-go, defer, or a separately planned narrow implementation with copy explicitly denying rights/
   master/approval implications.

## Human gate

Owner and counsel must approve a new implementation phase. This research plan cannot authorize recording.
