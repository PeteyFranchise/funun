# Phase 45.2: Writer's Room Session Recording — Research Context

**Captured:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** Research/legal review approved; recording implementation explicitly not approved
**Migration:** None

## Goal

Determine whether any Writer's Room session recording should exist after live-session adoption is known,
and if so what is recorded, with whose consent, for how long, at whose cost, and with what legal meaning.

## Locked boundaries

- **REC-01:** Recording, transcription, composition, and AI summaries remain disabled in Phase 44.1.
- **REC-02:** No covert/admin-default recording. Every participant receives explicit before-join and live
  indication; consent law and withdrawal behavior require counsel review.
- **REC-03:** Recording cannot silently become authorship/ownership/split/approval proof or a master.
- **REC-04:** Screen sharing may expose unreleased works, plugin licenses, messages, credentials, and other
  projects; scope/redaction/user control are required.
- **REC-05:** Resolve owner/custodian, participant access, download, deletion, retention, legal hold,
  export, block/removal, minors/regions, provider storage/encryption, breach response, and pricing.
- **REC-06:** Research may recommend no recording or narrow audio/clip/bookmark alternatives.
