<objective>
Rework the dashboard from a vanity scoreboard into an action surface (④): kill "Avg readiness", add an
action-oriented "Closest to ready" nudge, and add a "Your next moves" feed that is the home for
cross-account tasks — everything waiting on you, whoever owns the song, with money and signatures
always pinned on top so a contract is never buried.

notifications read or an AI pass, and feed it from sheets/documents reachable through BOTH owned and
shared projects (the one caller-side gap RESEARCH flags). Keep personal scoreboard math owner-only so
shared projects never inflate or distort the viewer's own catalog stats (③). Implements ④ and ③'s
scoreboard-exclusion half.

Output: a pure `lib/dashboard/next-moves.ts` (`buildNextMoves` with a locked pinned tier + a flexible
tier designed so per-user prefs can bolt on later), and the reworked dashboard.
</objective>

## Planner decisions

  `flexible` tier without touching `pinned`. No configurability UI ships now (CONTEXT Deferred Ideas).

  counter, sign a document. Action types beyond this (invites, audio-missing) are deferred.
- **③ coverage:** this plan covers the scoreboard-exclusion half of ③ (owner-scoped stats + owned-only
  "Closest to ready"); 21-03 covers the "Shared with me" lane half.
