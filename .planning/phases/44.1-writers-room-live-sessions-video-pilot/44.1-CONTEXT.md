# Phase 44.1: Writer's Room Managed Live Sessions — Context

**Captured:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** Owner-approved; managed-provider spike first
**Migration:** Unassigned/human-gated if session persistence is required; migration 228 unavailable

**Decision-time reference:**
`.planning/reviews/CODEX-NOTE-260923-zoom-video-sdk-vs-meeting-sdk.md` explains the two Zoom SDK families
and why Video SDK is the conditional fit if—and only if—the owner later selects Zoom.

## Goal

Add an on-demand room session for conversation, cameras, and DAW-window sharing without operating an SFU,
recording sessions, or treating WebRTC audio as a durable song asset.

## Locked decisions

- **LIVE-01:** Button is **Start a session**; nobody auto-joins and page presence is not billed as video.
- **LIVE-02:** Managed provider only. Daily, LiveKit, Twilio, and Zoom Video SDK are options, not a locked
  selection. Compare them and any then-credible alternative, then stop for an owner decision before
  installing or integrating a production SDK. Evaluate Zoom's customizable Video SDK, not Meeting SDK.
- **LIVE-03:** Maximum six participants initially and one active session per work.
- **LIVE-04:** Camera, microphone, device selection, audio-only mode, DAW-window screen share, participant
  strip, reconnect/network states, and accessible controls are required.
- **LIVE-05:** Screen share is visual/reference collaboration. Prefer application/window sharing and warn
  before whole-screen sharing.
- **LIVE-06:** Call/system audio is reference-quality. It cannot create a take, master, approval, custody,
  or rights fact.
- **LIVE-07:** Recording, transcription, compositions, PSTN, guests, backgrounds, and AI summaries are out.
- **LIVE-08:** Current room authorization is checked before token mint and revalidated on join/reconnect;
  removal/block ends access promptly.
- **LIVE-09:** Cost controls include feature flags, one active session, participant/duration limits, idle
  timeout, verified provider end events, usage reconciliation, alerts, and owner-visible reporting.
- **LIVE-10:** Camera/session presence is separate from green `Here now` room presence.

## Provider cost snapshot

Re-check before execution. On 2026-09-23 Daily listed 10,000 free video participant-minutes/month then
$0.004; LiveKit Ship listed $50/month including 150,000 WebRTC minutes and 250 GB downstream transfer;
Twilio listed $0.004/participant-minute. Zoom advertised 20 free Build Platform credits and flexible
credit plans, but its live credit-to-session-minute conversion still requires checkout/quote verification.
See the approved owner plan for direct official links and the limits on using these snapshots.

## Zoom-specific spike questions

- Confirm custom Funūn UI, token minting, webhook verification, usage reconciliation, and forced removal
  without falling back to Zoom Meeting UI or Zoom-hosted membership semantics.
- Test DAW-window and whole-screen capture across Chrome, Edge, Safari, macOS, and Windows. Zoom documents
  tab/system-audio sharing in Chrome and Edge; non-Chromium browsers cannot capture computer audio through
  `getDisplayMedia`, so unsupported states and copy must be explicit.
- Measure DAW playback with default speech processing and with Zoom's Web original-sound `hifi`/`stereo`
  options. Neither mode may be represented as a durable take or master without measured evidence.
- Measure bundle/runtime cost and the security/header implications of WebAssembly/`SharedArrayBuffer`
  paths versus WebRTC audio mode before selecting the integration shape.
- Keep Zoom cloud recording, transcript, summary, and saved provider chat capabilities disabled; their
  presence in the SDK does not change the Phase 45.2 legal gate.
