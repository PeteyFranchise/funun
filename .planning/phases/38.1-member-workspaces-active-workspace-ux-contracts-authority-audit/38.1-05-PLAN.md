# Phase 38.1-05 Plan — Evidence Expiry and Authority Lapse

## Scope

- Preserve the existing compute-on-read enforcement: expired, superseded, future-dated,
  undocumented, or unconfirmed evidence cannot support authority-tier permissions.
- Add one pure authority-status resolver that explains why an accepted relationship currently
  resolves to operational or authority access.
- Carry that status through the workspace roster read model and render a clear, neutral notice.
- Prove that expiry removes authority-tier permissions without removing operational permissions.

## Constraints

- No schema or production database change.
- No cron, stored authority tier, or cached permission set.
- Do not describe stored evidence as legally verified, approved, or interpreted by Funūn.
- Counsel review remains required for final user-facing document-state vocabulary.

## Verification

- Unit tests for exact expiry boundaries and precedence across mixed evidence rows.
- Room read-model tests for the new serializable status.
- Grant-service regression proving operational access survives while authority access lapses.
- Typecheck and targeted lint after implementation.
