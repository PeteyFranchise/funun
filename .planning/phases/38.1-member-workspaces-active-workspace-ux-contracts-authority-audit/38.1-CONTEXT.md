# Phase 38.1 Context — Member Workspaces UX, Contracts, Authority, and Audit

## Objective

Turn Phase 38's shipped workspace authorization layer into an explicit, safe Member experience.
The active workspace is carried by `/w/[workspaceId]/...`, resolved on the server for every request,
and always visible in the application chrome. Personal Member routes and the separate Funūn Team
Member sign-in boundary remain unchanged.

## Locked decisions inherited from Phase 38

- D-30: the URL is the source of active-workspace context; never browser storage or global client state.
- D-31: wrong-context protection requires both persistent workspace identity and a server-side
  acting-context check on every write.
- D-32: Management and Label workspaces land on Roster, with Activity as a sibling view.
- D-33: Member workspace switching is instant and keeps the same authenticated identity. The
  Personal/Funūn Team Member switch remains a hard sign-out/sign-in boundary.
- D-36 through D-39: authority is member-declared and evidence-supported; Funūn records provenance,
  never interprets legal effect; authority lapses on evidence expiry while operational access remains.
- D-41: rights-data changes made through a workspace are proposals until the Member confirms them.
- D-55/D-56: workspace surfaces remain behind the server-side cohort gate and global database switch.

## Phase sequence

1. Active workspace context: URL resolver, persistent chrome, instant in-session switcher, protected
   workspace landing route, and middleware coverage. Code-only; no schema or production mutation.
2. Roster and Activity sibling views backed by one consistent read model.
3. Member-visible workspace audit surface.
4. Contract Locker workspace shelf and canonical Member-document views, after counsel-approved copy.
5. Evidence expiry and authority-tier lapse enforcement.
6. Rights proposal and Member-confirmation workflow.
7. Master-ownership claims and evidence-derived label access.
8. Full automated verification, accessibility review, and deferred human UAT checklist.

## Non-goals for the first slice

- No production database changes or migrations.
- No workspace billing, plan enforcement, or general-availability rollout.
- No legal-state vocabulary beyond already-approved neutral workspace identity labels.
- No reuse of `AccountContextSwitch`; it changes authenticated identities and is intentionally the
  wrong abstraction for in-session Member workspace navigation.
- No client-side authorization and no active-workspace value in localStorage/sessionStorage.
