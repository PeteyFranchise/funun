<objective>
Give the split sheet a home it can be returned to, rebuilt against the identity/collaborator redesign. Today a saved draft is permanently unreachable — no list, no detail page, no edit mode, so PATCH /api/split-sheets/[id] has never had a caller (18-CONTEXT findings 1 and 2). This plan builds the living-draft surface — list, detail/edit page, a read-only share that is explicitly not a formal ask (P18-08), and change summaries when consensus resets (P18-09) — all of it respecting and explaining the freeze boundary already shipped in lib/split-sheets/lifecycle.ts rather than working around it (P18-06).

It supersedes the stale pre-redesign draft of this plan: the initiator is now party 1 automatically with a read-only, locked legal name and live-linked rights identity (deliberation §9, §1); collaborators are added through a NEW, separate fast-add picker (email/phone only, collapsed advanced info, pending/confirmed badge — deliberation §4/§6) rather than by extending the shared CollaboratorPicker unchanged; and the recipient can correct their own identity on the approval page (deliberation §7). It depends on the identity foundation (18-05): the live-identity resolver, the collaborators.status/legal_name columns, and the locked legal name.

Purpose: every other plan in this phase assumes a sheet can be looked at. The Locker's attention feed (18-02) links to sheets; attachment (18-03) acts on them. Without a reachable, editable sheet the rest of the phase points at nothing.
Output: three pure modules with tests, a widened list query, a share route, a §7 approval-identity action, three pages, a new PartyPicker, and SplitSheetBuilder taught to edit with an auto-included live-linked self row.
</objective>
