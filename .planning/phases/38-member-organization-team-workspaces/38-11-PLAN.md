<planner_decisions>
1. **This migration is pushed alone.** Migrations 182-185 are additive and may be batched. 186 edits
   live policies on five production tables, so it gets its own `supabase db push`, its own
   `migration list` confirmation and its own adversarial smoke run. The checkpoint text says so
   explicitly.
2. **Per-operation split, not a blanket `FOR ALL`.** The workspace branch is added to SELECT and
   UPDATE policies. It is deliberately NOT added to `vault_projects`' INSERT or DELETE policies —
   project creation by a workspace happens through a service route that writes the row as the
   subject Member (plan 38-12, D-24), and deletion of a Member's record is never a
   workspace-derived power.
3. **Kill switch — re-raised, not decided.** CONTEXT's risk 5 records that D-55 selected the cohort
   flag alone and recommends re-raising a platform-wide disable control at planning. This plan
   re-raises it in the owner checkpoint as an explicit question, and ships the helper with a single
   early-exit guard clause reading a `public.workspace_access_enabled()` predicate so a disable
   control can later be implemented as one function body change rather than a policy rewrite. The
   decision itself stays with the owner.
</planner_decisions>
