<objective>
Add the read-only "Appears on" shelf for records a Member contributed to but does not hold, and
implement two-sided record-custody transfer.

Purpose: D-27's shelf matters more for producers and engineers than for artists — a producer's
working life is largely records someone else holds. And D-29 is the one path in this phase by which
`vault_projects.user_id` can change hands, so it must be provably two-sided and provably narrow: it
moves custody and nothing else.

Output: `lib/workspaces/appears-on.ts` and `lib/workspaces/custody-transfer.ts` with paired tests,
the custody-transfer route, and a scoped extension to the Vault page's existing shared lane.
</objective>
