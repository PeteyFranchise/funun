| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-27-03 | Spoofing | forwarded deep-link onboarding a different email | low | mitigate | Token resolves pre-fill only; admission always re-derived from the submitted email at the gate + Supabase email confirmation guarantees inbox control (D-09) |
| T-27-04 | Denial of Service | token brute-force / route spam | high | mitigate | Shared rate limiter on both routes (ip dimension); service-client reads only |
| T-27-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | No new packages installed |
</threat_model>
