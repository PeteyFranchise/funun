# Phase 44.2: Writer's Room Synchronized Take Playback — Context

**Captured:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** Owner-approved; depends on stable protected take playback and Phase 44.1 session lifecycle
**Migration:** None expected

## Goal

Let collaborators audition one existing protected Writer's Room take together while a live session is
running, without routing the durable audio through the video provider.

## Locked decisions

- **SYNC-01:** Each authorized participant fetches the selected take through Funūn's protected audio path.
- **SYNC-02:** Realtime carries control state only: selected take/version, play/pause, seek, position,
  epoch/sequence, rate, and timing—not audio bytes.
- **SYNC-03:** One explicit host controls transport initially; host transfer and host-loss behavior are
  deterministic.
- **SYNC-04:** Reconnect/state request reconstructs authoritative transport and never resurrects a removed
  Member's access.
- **SYNC-05:** Drift is measured/corrected with bounded seek/playback-rate policy and visible “syncing”/
  degraded states. Do not claim sample-accurate DAW sync.
- **SYNC-06:** Private pins, unauthorized comments, masters, and other non-selected assets are untouched.
- **SYNC-07:** No playback action creates an approval, listen receipt, authorship, split, or rights fact.
