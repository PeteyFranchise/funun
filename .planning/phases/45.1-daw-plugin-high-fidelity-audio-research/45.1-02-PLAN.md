# Plan 45.1-02: High-fidelity live DAW-audio feasibility

## Objective

Determine whether Funūn should add a producer-friendly reference-audio path beyond browser screen-share
audio, without confusing it with durable upload or promising impossible remote-session fidelity.

## Research

1. Test current selected video-provider music/original-sound capabilities, stereo/bitrate/sample-rate,
   AGC/echo/noise controls, latency/jitter, headphones/feedback, CPU, and mixed browser/OS behavior.
2. Compare browser system/window capture, virtual audio device, Bridge-native capture, plugin-published
   track, and separate high-bitrate WebRTC path. Include setup burden, permission/privacy, support, cost,
   echo loops, failure, and mobile listener compatibility.
3. Conduct blinded producer listening for arrangement/reference versus mix-critical/master use; measure
   latency, artifacts, channel behavior, and dropouts. Preserve the invariant that uploaded takes are the
   durable source.
4. Threat-model unintended system audio, other sessions/notifications, DRM, device capture, provider
   processing/retention, and rights-sensitive unreleased material.
5. Return a no-go or separately scoped implementation recommendation with honest user-facing quality copy,
   support matrix, and cost forecast.

## Human gate

No original-sound/native-capture implementation without owner approval after the listening/security brief.
