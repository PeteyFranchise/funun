# Plan 45-01: Bridge architecture, threat model, and platform gate

## Objective

Resolve the desktop/security/storage choices before writing a native client or changing upload limits.

## Work

1. Survey beta-user DAW/OS evidence and producer workflows for Logic, Pro Tools, Ableton, FL Studio,
   Cubase/Studio One, bounce formats, session lengths, and current storage destinations.
2. Compare desktop stacks and update/signing paths for macOS and Windows; assess filesystem watchers,
   accessibility, crash reporting/privacy, secure keychain, notarization/code signing, auto-update,
   background behavior, and public-repo implications.
3. Compare browser-deep-link PKCE, loopback callback, and device-code-style auth against Supabase/current
   auth. Design least-privilege device/session scopes, rotation, revoke-all, lost-device, and recovery.
4. Threat-model malicious files, symlink/path traversal, partial/still-writing files, wrong-room delivery,
   token theft, local malware, upload replay, tampered metadata, duplicate bounces, offline queue, device
   sharing, auto-update compromise, support access, and unreleased-title/path leakage.
5. Define audio classes/formats/limits, content sniff/decoding, checksum, resumable chunks, quota owner,
   retention/local deletion, server orphan cleanup, and storage-ledger dependency. Measure representative
   WAV/AIFF/FLAC sizes and upload/retry times.
6. Prototype watched-folder completion detection and signed TUS upload against a disposable environment;
   do not connect production or persist credentials in source/plaintext.

## Human gate

Owner selects first OS/rollout sequence, desktop stack, auth approach, supported classes/formats/limits,
spool retention, auto-send posture, storage prerequisites, and beta cohort. No production client/server
implementation starts before this decision record.
