# The Crate's exclusivity term is counsel-gated — blocks one FAQ answer

**Captured:** 2026-09-25 · **Status:** blocked on counsel/BD, not on effort
**Blocks:** the marketing page FAQ question *"What changes if a song goes into The Crate?"*

## What the agreement actually says

`lib/sync-library/agreement.ts`:

> **"[PLACEHOLDER — exclusivity of representation]: whether Funūn's representation under this
> Agreement is exclusive or non-exclusive is to be decided with counsel/BD. Default proposed for
> review: non-exclusive, unless a specific Song/deal is made exclusive per Section 4."**

And the file header:

> **"pre-authorizes Funūn to shop AND negotiate/execute sync licenses on the artist's behalf —
> EXCEPT price, scope, medium, and exclusivity, which are negotiated per deal."**

So two separate facts: the *representation* model is undecided, and *per-deal* exclusivity is
explicitly permitted by Section 4.

## Why this cannot be answered yet

An exclusivity statement on a public page is a contractual representation, not marketing copy. If
counsel lands on exclusive representation — or a single placement is sold as exclusive — every
artist who signed up reading "we don't do exclusive deals" was told something untrue about their
rights. In a rights product that is the most expensive class of error there is.

`docs/marketing/funun-launch-copy-brief.md` does describe The Crate as a *"curated, non-exclusive
sync catalog"*, but that is a **creative brief**, not ratified terms. It cannot settle this.

## What IS safe to say, and already is

**Core product use grants Funūn nothing.** The Writer's Room, Sound Vault and Release Report
involve no agreement, no representation and no exclusivity — the sync agreement is opt-in and
separate. The FAQ says this plainly, and it should not be hedged.

**Scoping note, learned the hard way:** a narrow contractual uncertainty about one optional feature
must not be allowed to shadow the whole product. The first draft of the FAQ answer did exactly
that, and made Funūn sound encumbered when nothing is.

## Unblocks when

Counsel/BD rules on exclusivity of representation. Then write the Crate FAQ entry, and revisit the
blanket agreement's placeholder text at the same time — they are the same decision.
