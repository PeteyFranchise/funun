# Bulk catalogue intake for The Crate

**Captured:** 2026-10-04 · **Status:** roadmapped, deliberately deferred
**Owner framing:** *"we will have to build a bulk intake path eventually for this purpose, but
later and add it to the roadmap"*

## Why it is deferred rather than dropped

The submit path takes **one song at a time, up to three per submission** (owner decision,
2026-10-04). That is correct for a writer with a song. Someone arriving with a catalogue of
hundreds cannot use it, and is likely a larger opportunity than anyone it was designed for.

The interim answer is **not** a bulk upload: the song they attached is reviewed normally, and the
catalogue behind it is **flagged for a Funūn Team Member to pick up as a conversation**.

## The thing that must be settled BEFORE any bulk path is built

**Authority, not scale.** A manager or label submitting songs they do not personally own may not
be entitled to make them licensable. Funūn's position is that the artist owns the song and Funūn
represents it; a submitter who merely represents a catalogue sits on that line.

Open questions a bulk path cannot dodge:

- Who signs the agreement when the submitter is not the owner?
- What evidence of authority is required, and who verifies it?
- Who is paid, and against what record?
- What happens when one artist in a represented catalogue objects?

**Building volume intake before these are answered would let songs reach buyers on an agreement
signed by someone who may not have been entitled to sign it.** That is the money-bug shape
`.claude/CLAUDE.md` warns about, at scale rather than one song at a time.

The flow already asks ownership outright and early (*"are these your songs, or do you represent
them?"*), so the signal exists before the volume does.
